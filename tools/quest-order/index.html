How this order is chosen. The sequence is the OSRS Wiki Optimal quest guide, read verbatim from the Quest Helper plugin. It is a community-curated recommendation, not a computed optimum — it minimises back-tracking and keeps prerequisite chains intact, but "best" always depends on what's reachable for your account (see Leagues below). Requirement data is extracted from Quest Helper's start-gates; rows marked ⚠ have extra entry requirements (varbit/item/sub-quest states) not captured here.
