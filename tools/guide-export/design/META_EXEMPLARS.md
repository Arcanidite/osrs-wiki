## 2. How it validates + sharpens our model
- **Epochs (OPPORTUNISTIC_GRANULARITY §2-epoch) confirmed by practice:** the account-build
  SPINE (macroefficient, QoL-first) is distinct from loot/PvM GRIND epochs. Oziris v4's whole
  premise (rush access to CG, then grind CG as its own loop) is the spine-vs-loot-epoch split.
- **Goals = QoL/unlock milestones (progression-philosophy) confirmed:** the meta's "essential"
  compounding unlocks — Piety, Berserker ring, Bow of Faerdhinen (near-essential), fairy rings,
  graceful, barrows gloves, herb sack, void — are exactly the opportunistic weave's early-fetch
  payoff targets.
- **CG-rush = a goal-variant** with a different demand horizon (rush CG access, sacrifice
  well-roundedness) — a lens/goal-set, not new machinery.
