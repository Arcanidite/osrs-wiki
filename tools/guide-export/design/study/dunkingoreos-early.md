# DunkingOreos Early Ironman/HCIM Guide — Structure & Requisites

## Section Hierarchy

**Phase 1: Ardougne Cloak (Steps 1–35)**
- Checkpoint 1A: Lumbridge & Port Sarim Foundation (vendor capital, initial quests, basic item access)
- Checkpoint 1B: Kandarin Unlock (Barbarian Assault minigame, eastern routes, cake thieving to 21 Thieving)
- Checkpoint 1C: Asgarnia Base (western hub, POH placement, combat quest chain, farming access)
- Checkpoint 1D: Diary Completion (minigame hubs, Ardougne Easy Diary reward: cloak teleport)

**Phase 2: Minigames & Combat Quests (Steps 1–9)**
- Tempoross: Supplies (food, sapphire necklaces, construction XP via massing)
- Wintertodt: Logs (12 magic logs, 75 Firemaking, additional XP streams)
- Early Combat Quests: Basic combat unlocks (Fight Arena, Waterfall, Tree Gnome Village, Dragon Slayer I)

**Phase 3: Skilling Grinds (Steps 1–20)**
- Graceful Outfit (Canifis Rooftop 260 marks → QoL travel efficiency)
- Fossil Island Access (Bone Voyage prerequisites, mid-tier resource unlocks)

**Phase 4: Fairy Rings & Thieving (Steps 1–9)**
- Fairytale I → Fairy Ring unlock (free fast-travel network)
- Thieving progression: Fruit stalls → Blackjacking → Wealthy citizens (50+ Thieving)

**Phase 5: Recipe for Disaster (Steps 1–35)**
- RFD Subquests (6 prerequisite quests: Goblins, Mountain Dwarf, Pirate Pete, Guide, Evil Dave, Skrach)
- Intermediate Quest Chain (cat quests, Underground Pass, Monkey Madness, Legends, Hero, Fremennik)
- RFD Finale (6 boss encounters, escalating difficulty)

## Interleaving & Preparation Wisdom (CRITICAL)

### Skill Interleaving & Batching

**Multiquest Chains Sequenced by Prerequisites:**
- **Demon Slayer path:** Requires 20 bones (gather from Hill Giant drops or kill chickens/imps) → feed to Sanfew (get pestle/mortar) → unlock herblore requirements later. Bones collected *during* unrelated hills grind (Hill Giants for Thieving marks); cached in bank.
- **Cat quest series (Evil Dave, Ratcatchers, A Tail of Two Cats, Ichlarin's Little Helper):** All require fully grown cat from kitten. Kitten maturation runs passively during Tempoross/Wintertodt grinds (no active task cost). Enables quest chain only when cat ready (Step 18+), preventing backtrack.
- **RFD subquest batching:** Six subquests collected in sequence (Goblins → Dwarf → Pirate Pete → Guide → Evil Dave → Skrach → Finale). Guide step (cooking/magic) and Pirate Pete (fishing) require Fishing/Cooking prerequisites met *before* subquest entry, preventing replay; batch cooking training (Mess Hall 70 Cooking) immediately before Sir Amik Varze subquest entry.

### Background Tasks & Cadence

**Continuous parallel tasks (no interruption cost):**
- **Kitten raising:** Passively matures during minigame grinds; no active management required.
- **Farming runs:** Compost bin pre-placed (Falador or via leprechaun in Step 9). Rake, compost, plant in single trip between quest clusters. Pineapples planted at intro; only *weeds harvested* in later cycles (for Ratcatchers herb supply).
- **Birdhouse runs:** Skill level low-requirement (Step 27 implies 10 Thieving sufficient for seed collection); run between major quest gates to stockpile seeds for future allotment unlocks.
- **Prayer/Food Stockpile:** Big bones from Hill Giants buried for Prayer XP during strike; raw meats collected in bulk (5+ raw) before quests requiring cooked meals; banking consolidated to single Edgeville trip per 5 quests.

**Ahead-of-time vs Just-in-time:**
- **Ahead-of-Time:** Sapphire necklaces (crafted from Tempoross drops early; enchanted to Games Necklace before Section 3 to enable minigame teleports). Dueling rings (crafted 10+ and enchanted before PvP Arena visits). Digsite pendants (2–3 pre-made for zone teleportation). All consumables for quest chains (stews, wines, bread, fish) collected in bulk 1 quest *before* usage.
- **Just-in-Time:** Dyes crafted only after HAM pickpocketing secured redberries/woad/onions. Molten glass made *on-demand* when Crafting needed for Dwarf Cannon or other single-use steps. Antifire shield (obtained Step 35 *immediately* before RFD Finale, never stored early).

### Train-to-Breakpoint Rules

**Agility progression:** Train rooftop courses only to level gates (40 Agility unlocks Graceful accumulation start; 60 Agility grants rare magic log drop from Wintertodt teak chops—*defer intensive Woodcutting grind until 60 Agility hit*). No overtraining before prerequisites cascade.

**Fishing progression:** Minimum 58 Fishing for Tempoross solo capacity; extend to 70+ *only if* time permits (Massing mode grants faster rewards per real-time). No rush to 80+ (stockpiles from Tempoross/Wintertodt suffice for quests).

**Crafting progression:** Reach 27 Crafting (Tempoross drops) → craft sapphire amulets immediately (avoid double-pass for necklace crafting). Hit 40 Crafting (molten glass production) to feed jewelry enchanting (Games Necklace from Tempoross drops require 40+ Crafting for string conversion). 50+ Crafting only for RFD subquests (Evil Dave requires dragonstone jewelry).

### Ordering Rules Implied

1. **Diary completion before minigames:** Ardougne Easy Diary (Step 32) must finish *before* relying on Ardougne Cloak teleport for efficient minigame access (Tempoross, Trawler Charter). Phase 1 is prerequisite gating, not optional.

2. **Tempness → Wintertodt → Teak grind sequence:** Tempoross (early) provides necklaces + food. Wintertodt (mid, 50+ Firemaking) yields magic logs and further construction XP. Teak chops (post-Wintertodt, 50+ Woodcutting, ideally 60+ Agility) are background cadence, not critical path.

3. **POH placement strategy:** Move from Rimmington (early artisan hub) → Hosidius (Woodcutting Guild access, mid-game) → Pollnivneach (post-Bone Voyage, late-game QoL). Prevent wasted teleports.

4. **Thieving unlock order:** Cake stealing (20 Thieving at Step 11) for proto-income. Fruit stalls (42+ Thieving, Step 3B) for better yields. Blackjacking (50 Thieving, Step 4) as full transition. Wealthy citizens (53+ Thieving, Section 4) only for sweetcorn gathering (not income).

5. **Combat quest prerequisites before RFD finale:** All intermediate quests (Monkey Madness, Legends, Hero, Fremennik) *must* complete before RFD finale (Step 35). Finale requires specific greegrees (Monkey Madness drops) and combat item prerequisites (Underground Pass → Iban's Staff for clue step). No shortcutting via skipping "optional" intermediates.

## Self-Sufficiency & Gathering Model

### Initial Capital & Income Paths

**Phase 1 Capital:** Tutorial item vendor sales (initial 1–2k gp) + Stronghold of Security floors 1–3 (10k gp one-time). Total usable: ~10–12k gp before *any* gathering.

**Phase 1 Income (capped until Thieving 20):** NO Grand Exchange buying. First ~5k gp spent on *necessities* (staff of air, steel axe, mind runes in bulk). Deficit covered by cake stealing (Step 11 onward, 5 gp per cake, 30+ cakes bankable for ~150 gp per run). Silk thieving (50 gp per silk to Silk Merchant, Steps 11–12) provides income surge (15 silk = 750 gp).

### Consumable Self-Sufficiency

**Food:** No purchases except cakes (thieved) and pineapples (spawned from compost bin). Cooking progression:
- Early: raw meat from cow/chicken kills, cooked on kitchen fires (Lumbridge, Ardougne).
- Mid: raw swordfish + sardine obtained from purchases (Port Sarim fish vendor) but only *one inventory*, then fished ad-hoc via Fishing quests (Fishing Contest, Fishing Trawler) or fishing spots (Barbarian Village trout, Cathery tuna).
- Late: karambwans (fished from Karamja via Tai Bwo Wannai Trio access, not bought) combined with Cooking (swordfish for solo, karambwan for combo). Lobsters (fished or claimed from minigame rewards).

**Potions & Herbal Supplies:**
- Antipoison: 1 purchased (Step 33 from Jiminua for ritual poison test); subsequent antipoisons crafted via herblore (30+ herb supply from HAM pickpocketing, cadava berries, volencia moss) or obtained from Fishing Trawler Charter (buckets of slime, soda ash).
- Prayer Restoration: Bones buried (Hill Giant drops, tree chopping necromancy-like collection during woodcutting). No paid prayer restoration potions; rely on prayer flicking or ectoplasm altar (one-time entry cost via Ghosts Ahoy).
- Super Restore & Restore: Desert Treasure requires potions (4–dose bottles crafted post-herblore unlock, OR purchased from Mage Training Arena via rune gambling; see MTA entry below).

**Runes & Magical Supplies:**
- Air/Mind Runes: Bulk purchased from Aubury (Port Sarim wizard vendor, ~10k gp bulk buy). No crafting route viable early (Rune Mysteries grants 32 Runecrafting via lamp; active runecrafting too slow).
- Cosmic Runes: Purchased from Mage Bank (50+ reserve for jewelry enchanting across Sections 2–5).
- Law/Nature Runes: Mage Bank purchase (law for teleport backbone, nature for telekinetic Grasp in quests).
- Elemental Runes: Bulk purchased or claimed from Tempoross/Wintertodt reward chests.
- Blood Rune (1): Purchased from Ali Morrisane post-Feud completion (Runecrafting too slow; 50+ required for self-sufficiency via abyss).

**Arrows & Ammo:**
- Bronze arrows: Fletched (1,005 shafts → 1,005 arrows, Steps 1–2, 18, 20-plus). Arrowtips purchased once (1,005 bronze arrowtips, Step 16). No arrow self-sufficiency; reliant on fletching / feather gathering.
- Bone bolts (1,000): Purchased from Boneyard merchant post-Lost City (via Aircut general store). No crafting route before Dragon Slayer I.
- Ogre arrows (20+): Crafted via Big Chompy Bird Hunting (headless + feather + ogre shafts; shafts are foraged resource, not bought).

**Gear & Armor:**
- Graceful Outfit (260 marks via rooftop agility): Free via rooftop courses, not purchased. Replaces Agility Pyramid cost entirely post-Step 11.
- Combat Gear (coif, chaps, vambs, green d'hide body): Purchased from shops (Champion's Guild, Horvik in Varrock) but only *single set* needed; no duplicate purchases or replacements until late-game slayer.
- Enchanted Jewelry (Games Necklace, Dueling Ring, Digsite Pendant, Necklace of Passage): Crafted from raw materials → silver bars (smelted) or gems (cut) → enchanted with cosmos runes. Zero vendor purchases (all self-produced).
- Magic Logs & Construction: Supplied via Wintertodt and teak grinding (Sections 2B–3B). No logging vendor.

### Group-Gathered Resources (Minigames)

**Tempoross (Solo or Mass):**
- Solo: ~58 Fishing required; slower (~2 min per kill), but yields all individual rewards per kill (permits, molten lava, supplies).
- Massing: Faster real-time per kill, shared rewards; prioritize solo until sapphire necklace RNG satisfied, then consider mass for faster supply velocity if time permits.
- Critical drops: Sapphire necklaces (craft into games necklaces if drops insufficient; see Crafting fallback above).

**Wintertodt (Solo or Mass):**
- Solo: ~50 Firemaking to start; solo runs ~1 hour for 12 magic logs (not fast but reliable).
- Mass: Faster per-kill firemaking XP and additional construction/fletching XP; coordinated team preferred late-phase (after supply stockpile from Tempoross).
- Critical resource: Magic logs (12 minimum, kept; additional logs for teak greening or late-game construction if needed).

**Fishing Trawler:**
- Partial participation (1 game launch per visit, immediate leave): Grants charter access without engaging full minigame loop.
- Yields: Crab meat (RFD Pirate Pete), kelp (RFD Pirate Pete), charter teleport discount (reduce trips to Catherby).
- Soda ash & buckets of sand: Obtainable from charter vendor (alternative to crafting).

**Barbarian Assault:**
- Minigame teleport unlocked via dialogue (skip tutorial with Captain Cain).
- Zero resource yield; purely for teleport access (saves travel time to Kandarin).

**Soul Wars & LMS (Last-Man-Standing):**
- Pure teleport hubs; no gathering. Intermediate waypoints for minigame cluster visits (Barbarian Assault, Fishing Trawler, Edge portal access).

## Steer Points & Milestones

### Non-Level, Non-Quest Steer Points (Priority Flags)

1. **Ardougne Easy Diary Completion (Step 32, Phase 1D):** Unlocks Ardougne Cloak (teleport anchor for all remaining phases). *Not a level or quest alone*; requires 6+ quest completions + 20 Thieving + Diary task progression. Gate: all phases depend on cloak efficiency.

2. **Barbarian Assault Minigame Unlock (Step 15, Phase 1B):** Unlocks minigame teleport hubs (links to Soul Wars, LMS, Fishing Trawler, others). Gate: enables fast travel for Sections 2–5.

3. **Graceful Outfit (Phase 3A, Step 11):** Collect 260 marks of grace from rooftop courses. Unlock: 60 weight reduction → 67% run energy retention (QoL, not stat-based). Gate: rooftop grind becomes feasible post-Graceful.

4. **Fossil Island Access (Phase 3B, Step 9):** Bone Voyage quest completion + mushtree teleports. Unlock: island-based slayer training, mushroom farming, fossil island locked chests. Gate: mid-to-late game skilling hubs.

5. **Fairy Rings Unlock (Phase 4, Step 7):** Fairytale I quest completion (no stat requirements). Unlock: free fast-travel network (connects to all major resource hubs). Gate: late-game convenience, though not strictly necessary before RFD.

6. **POH Relocation Milestone (Phases 1C → 3B → 4):** Move POH from Rimmington (early artisan hub) → Hosidius (Woodcutting Guild proximity) → Pollnivneach (late-game minigame clustering). Gate: each move saves 10–30 seconds per POH teleport re-entry.

### Level-Based Steer Points

- **15 Firemaking (Step 1):** Unlock cooking on fires (bypass early kitchen spawn dependency).
- **20 Agility (Step 18):** Unlock Draynor Rooftop (sustained marks grinding).
- **21 Thieving (Step 11):** Unlock silk stealing (income surge pre-Graceful).
- **40 Agility (Step 8, Section 3A):** Unlock Graceful outfit accumulation (gate for Phase 3A completion).
- **42 Crafting (Step 3, Section 5):** Unlock fishbowl crafting (RFD Mountain Dwarf subquest requirement).
- **50 Firemaking (Section 2B):** Unlock Wintertodt entry (solo viable; 75 Firemaking required for quest cape).
- **50 Thieving (Phase 4, Step 4):** Unlock blackjacking (higher income than fruit stalls).
- **58 Fishing (Section 2A):** Unlock solo Tempoross (supplies + necklaces).
- **65 Attack/Strength/Defence (Phase 5, Step 34):** Gate for RFD Finale (combat stats check; exact requirements vary by boss).
- **75 Firemaking (Section 2B):** Quest cape prerequisite (quelled-agility-push milestone, optional for early-game gate).

### Quest Steer Points

- **Dragon Slayer I (Phase 2C, Step 5):** Unlock antifire shield (stat gate for later dragon encounters). Crandor map obtained; Elvarg fought on Crandor island.
- **Waterfall Quest (Phase 1B–2C, Steps 15–7):** Unlock 30 Attack + 30 Strength XP lamp (accelerates combat training).
- **Monkey Madness I (Phase 5, Step 21):** Unlock greegrees (required for RFD Awowogei, Legends Quest finale). Four-trip resource acquisition (talismans + bones per monkey type).
- **Desert Treasure (Phase 5, Step 32):** Unlock Ancients spellbook + 61 Magic XP lamp. Four-part dungeon gauntlet (Fareed, Damis, Dessous, Kamil) safespottable. Gate: later-game spell unlocks (spellbook swap; not strictly required but enables Morytania boss optimizations).
- **Underground Pass (Phase 5, Step 20):** Unlock Iban's Staff + Iban's upgraded staff path (also required for Regicide prep in Monkey Madness II, not covered in this guide).
- **Recipe for Disaster Finale (Phase 5, Step 35):** Gate: all combat, skilling, and quest prerequisites. Six escalating boss encounters (each requires specific item + prayer/gear combos).

### Access & Unlock Steer Points

- **Zeah Unlocked (Phase 1A, Step 7):** Unlock Kourend Client quest entry. Grants Hosidius house relocation (mid-game efficiency).
- **Karamja Unlocked (Phase 5, Step 33):** Unlock Tai Bwo Wannai Trio (karambwan fishing, greenhorn shortcut, musa point pub for Barcrawl).
- **Entrana Unlocked (via Lost City quest):** Unlock Holy Grail quest path. Entrana is prayer restoration via prayer altar (one-time ectoplasm entry cost in Ghosts Ahoy).
- **Mage Bank (Wilderness, minigame teleport shortcut):** Unlock cosmic rune + rune bulk purchases (90+ runes per visit for jewelry enchanting chain).
- **Port Khazard (via Hazeel Cult, Step 10):** Unlock Tindel's shop (Clue Hunter gear identification). Charter ship vendor (alternative to Catherby for soda ash/sand).
- **Burthorpe (via minigame teleport or games neck):** Unlock Rogue's Den (lockpick training, rogue outfit). Slayer task master (Turael for early quest-compatible tasks).

## Efficiency Heuristics Summary

1. **Diary completion as transport anchor:** Ardougne Easy Diary (Step 32) unlocks the single most-reused teleport (Ardougne Cloak) for subsequent phases. All minigame hubs, quests, and resource areas anchor to this.

2. **Kitten maturation as passive background:** Raise from kitten during Tempoross/Wintertodt grinds; no active management. Enables cat quest series only when fully grown (Step 18+).

3. **Consumable batching pre-quest:** Collect all food/potions/runes *before* quest entry to prevent quest-internal backtracks. Stockpile 100+ bread/fish, 20+ wines, 50+ mind/air runes per major quest chain.

4. **POH placement cascades:** Rimmington (early artisan) → Hosidius (woodcutting guild proximity) → Pollnivneach (late-game minigame access). Each move saves cumulative seconds/day.

5. **Skill synergy windows:** Firemaking & Woodcutting interleave via Tempoross → Wintertodt → teak grinding. Thieving & Agility run parallel (rooftop courses while thieving cakes/silk). Crafting demands follow jewelry enchanting demand (sapphire from Tempoross, molten glass from soda ash, cosmic runes from Mage Bank).

6. **Travel hierarchy:** Ardougne Cloak → Minigame Teleports (LMS, Soul Wars, Barbarian Assault, Trawler) → Chartered Ships → Spirit Trees → Fairy Rings. Leverage each tier's efficiency before next.

7. **Safespot identification:** Multiple quests leverage safespots (black demon behind tree in Grand Tree, mother behind rock in Horror From The Deep, Zealots in Underground Pass). Document puzzles and routes externally to avoid quest-internal re-solve time loss.

8. **Quest helper dependency:** External guides mandatory for complex route puzzles (Monkey Madness talismans, Desert Treasure prayer/spell combos, RFD boss-specific gear combos). Build once per world; replicate per account.
