# Bossing Ladder — Progression Model

## Phase Hierarchy

### Easy Tier (Entry)
- F2P quests + basic combat intro
- Stat gates: Attack/Strength/Defence 40+
- Quest unlock: Dragon Slayer I (anti-dragon shield)
- Representative bosses: Obor, Bryophyta, Barrows, Giant Mole, Wintertodt
- Gear checkpoint: Rune equipment + anti-dragon shield
- Prayer unlock: 43+ via Dragon bones (preparation ahead-of-time)

### Medium Tier (Multi-mechanic intro)
- Mid-quest chains: Monkey Madness I, Desert Treasure I, RFD
- Stat gates: Attack/Strength/Defence 65+
- Unlocks: Ancient Magicks, Barrows gloves
- Representative bosses: King Black Dragon, Kraken, God Wars Dungeon (teams)
- Gear checkpoint: Dragon equipment (upgrade from Rune)
- Mechanic intro: Overhead prayers, Inventory management, Aggression

### Hard Tier (Solid sequences)
- Complex quest chains: Song of the Elves, Monkey Madness II, While Guthix Sleeps
- Stat gates: Attack/Strength/Defence 75+; Ranged/Magic 70+
- Gear checkpoint: Mid-tier equipment; Prifddinas unlock
- Representative bosses: Dagannoth Kings, Demonic gorillas, Abyssal Sire
- Mechanics: Tribrid combat styles, Phase mechanics, Minion management

### Elite Tier (High precision)
- Quest unlock: Desert Treasure II completion + Beneath Cursed Sands
- Stat gates: Combat stats 80+
- Representative bosses: Zulrah, Vorkath, Tombs of Amascut
- Mechanics: Advanced attack patterns, Hybrid switches, Enrage intro

### Master Tier (Long precision)
- Stat gates: Combat 90+; primary stats 95+
- Representative bosses: Chambers of Xeric, Theatre of Blood, TzKal-Zuk
- Mechanics: Extended encounter supply management, Enrage mastery

### Grandmaster Tier (Cosmetic aspirational)
- Stat gates: Max combat 95+ all
- Content: Challenge Mode Chambers, Hard Mode Theatre, Yama's Contracts, Grandmaster Achievements

**Format**: [Phase] [Stat requirement] [Mechanic focus] [Representative challenge]

## Interleaving & Prep Wisdom

### Stat Progression Breakpoints (Critical Sequencing)
- **40 Combat (Attack/Strength/Defence)**: Enables Rune gear + early quests; gate to Easy tier
- **43 Prayer**: MUST be trained AHEAD-OF-TIME (via Dragon bones burial or early slayer) before entering Easy tier with combat; overhead prayer unlocks half-AFKable early bossing
- **65 Combat**: Gate to Dragon equipment (Monkey Madness I, Desert Treasure I, RFD unlock); Ancient Magicks available (batches Magic-training with quests)
- **75 Combat**: Gate to hard-tier bosses; raids via Fight Caves Fire cape prerequisite
- **80+ Combat**: Elite tier viable; narrower tuning window; most content becomes skill-gated vs stat-gated
- **90+/95 Combat**: Master tier; precision > stats

### Prayer as Backstitch
- Early prayer (43) must be **obtained ahead-of-time** via slayer tasks (drags/bonecruchers) or quest/diary rewards; cannot JIT at combat tiers
- Prayer switches become active mechanic at 65+ tier (God Wars intro); by Master tier, switching under pressure is routine
- Training prayer early enables **background skill progression**: bury bones while doing quests, slayer, gathering

### Batched Quest Chains (Avoid Scattering)
- **Monkey Madness line**: Monkey Madness I (65 Combat gate) → Monkey Madness II (75+ gate); both unlock combat unlocks (Barrows gloves tier-up, godspell progression); do as pair to compress travel
- **Desert Treasure line**: Desert Treasure I (65+ gate, Ancient Magicks unlock) → Desert Treasure II (Elite gate, Awakened spells); batch together for magic unlock continuity
- **Song of Elves chain**: Prerequisite for Prifddinas (mid-tier gear vendor, crystalline armor, seed drop rate); marks hard-tier entry gate; do BEFORE combat stat grind past 70

### Supply Runs & Background Cadences
- **Food & potions**: Easy-Medium tiers are JIT from fishing/cooking runs during downtime; Master tier requires **ahead-of-time batch** farming (farm/Miscellania/GotR ritual/Kingdom)
- **Prayer potions**: Herblore unlock at ~52 (Ranarr); Medium tier onward requires sustained supply; background production via farm + Miscellania
- **Runes for spellcasting**: Runecrafting (Magic unlocked post-65) scales per tier; Master tier raids require stockpiled rune supplies; prep JIT via runecrafting or talisman trades
- **Ammunition & alch supply**: Fletching scales; high-tier PvM requires fletched ammo; batch with woodcutting runs or AFK alching

### Gear Interleaving (Avoid Gear-chase Scattering)
- **Easy tier**: Accumulate Barrows drops over many runs; full set sufficient, not optimal
- **Medium tier**: Dragon armor drop from slayer/KBD; batch with Combat training to justify time
- **Hard tier**: Mid-tier uniques (Barrows upgrade, Zenyte shard crafts) from slayer & hard-tier bosses; **run slayer ALONGSIDE** boss progression to sustain supply
- **Elite/Master**: BiS items drop from the tier itself; no separate gear-chase needed; tier IS the gear-unlock mechanism

### Mechanic Learning Progression (Gating Rules)
- **Safespotting only** (Easy) → **overhead prayers** (Medium intro) → **prayer switches** (Medium advanced) → **tribrid switches** (Hard) → **rapid switches under enrage** (Master)
- Do NOT skip tiers; attempting Hard without Medium prayer-switch practice (God Wars teams) → punishing wipes and supply waste
- **Minion management** (Easy simple adds) → **complex multi-minion phases** (Hard+ Nex/Sire) scales with phase count

### Diary & Diaries-as-Unlock Gates
- Hard Diaries unlock region speed-ups (bank teleports, resource access) that enable longer-session play; tier them with boss progression (not separately)

## Self-Sufficiency & Gathering

### Food (Fishing + Cooking)
- **Easy tier**: Herring/Mackerel (cooking 10-15); cooked during downtime on-site or JIT
- **Medium tier**: Lobbies/Tuna (cooking 40-50); sustained via fishing runs between boss sessions
- **Hard/Elite tier**: Monkfish (cooking 70+); requires ahead-of-time stockpile OR background fishing during other activities (farm, kingdom, GotR ritual)
- **Master tier**: Shark/Anglerfish (cooking 80-94); master tier requires JIT batch farming because supply consumption is high

### Prayer Potions (Herblore + Farming)
- Ranarr (Herblore 52) from farm harvest; batch cultivation ahead-of-time
- Training Herblore: Medium tier grind, optional background; Master tier raids necessitate it
- Supply model: **ahead-of-time farm batch** (1-2 runs) provides 20+ sessions' stock

### Runes (Runecrafting)
- Magic-training post-65 introduces runecrafting need
- **Talisman trade route** (e.g., cosmic runes from crafters) is JIT alternative to reduce runecrafting grind
- Medium tier: Staff-based casts reduce runecrafting load (Ancient staff); prepare staffs ahead-of-time
- Master tier raids: Rune consumption spikes; batch runecrafting runs (or talismans) into prep week

### Ammunition (Fletching + Gathering)
- Arrows/Bolts scale with Ranged tier; Easy-Medium: JIT bodkin/broad bolts (buyable, production minimal)
- Hard/Elite: Dragon darts/arrows; requires **ahead-of-time fletching batch** OR slayer drops
- Fletching scales from Woodcutting; batch woodcutting runs into fletching to sustain supply

### Armor & Weapons (Crafting + Slayer Drop)
- **Easy/Medium**: Barrows drops provide armor; accumulate over runs (no crafting needed)
- **Hard tier**: Mid-tier uniques from slayer (Zenyte shard crafts into jewelry, armor); crafting optional
- **Elite/Master**: Gear drops FROM the tier bosses (Tbow, Scythe); no separate gathering phase; tier completion IS gear-unlock

### Potions (Herblore + Farming)
- Stamina potions: Herblore 79 (high gate); Master tier necessity
- Training model: **JIT during Medium-tier grinding**; by Elite tier, production automated via farm
- Bastion/Battlemage potions: Herblore 80+; also Master-tier prep
- Supply: ahead-of-time farm batch during downtime

## Steer-Point Orientation

### Level-Gated Steer Points (Hard progression locks)
- Prayer 43 — enables overhead prayer; locks progression start
- Combat 65 — Dragon equipment tier unlock; gate to Medium-tier bosses
- Combat 75 — Hard-tier bosses viable; Fire Caves/Fire cape gate to raids
- Combat 80+ — Elite tier accessible; narrow stat margin; skill/mechanics primary thereafter
- Combat 95 (primary stats) — Master tier; cosmetic final tier

### Quest-Locked Steer Points (Access unlocks)
- Dragon Slayer I — anti-dragon shield (early combat requirement)
- Monkey Madness I — Barrows gloves upgrade (65 combat gate)
- Desert Treasure I — Ancient Magicks unlock (65 combat gate)
- Recipe for Disaster — Barrows gloves final (65 combat gate)
- Song of the Elves — Prifddinas access (hard-tier prep)
- Monkey Madness II — Blessed saradomin sword unlock (75+ gate)
- While Guthix Sleeps — Ancient curses unlock (hard-tier prep)
- Dragon Slayer II — Vorkath access (elite-tier specific)
- Desert Treasure II — Awakened spells (elite-tier specific)
- Beneath Cursed Sands — Tomb of Amascut (elite-tier specific)

### Gear-Tier Steer Points (Non-level unlocks)
- Rune full set — Easy tier baseline
- Dragon full set — Medium tier baseline
- Barrows pieces (accumulation) — Medium tier; earned via runs, not tiered purchase
- Zenyte jewelry (crafted) — Hard tier; earned from slayer via Zenyte shard
- Prifddinas armor (crystalline) — Hard tier unlock via Song of Elves + crafting/purchasing
- Fire cape — Hard tier unlock; raid gate via Fight Caves
- Twisted bow, Scythe of vitur — Master tier drops (self-supplied via raiding)
- Challenge Mode ornaments — Grandmaster cosmetic

### Minigame/Activity Unlock Steer Points (Non-combat)
- Pest Control (unlocked at Combat 40): Optional ranged training; Medium tier supports it
- Barbarian Assault (unlocked at Combat 40): Optional mage/range; scales per tier
- Fight Caves: Combat 70+ entry; Fire cape gate to hard-tier raids (Barrows gloves + Fire cape → raids)
- God Wars Dungeon: 75+ Combat + appropriate gear; learn prayer switches (Medium-tier progression)
- Zulrah: 78+ ranged/magic; Elite tier gate; first hybrid boss

### Collection Log / Unique Drops (Cosmetic steer progression)
- Barrows uniques (12 pieces) — Easy-Medium tier; cosmetic log milestone
- God Wars uniques (Godsword blades) — Medium tier; iconic milestone
- Zenyte shards (4 types) — Hard tier; jewelry grind milestone
- Elite/Master boss uniques (Tbow, Scythe, etc.) — Self-defined by raid tier
- Cosmetic ornaments (Challenge Mode) — Grandmaster aspirational

### Region / Dungeon Access Gates (Non-level, practical)
- Lumbridge Swamp (Barrows) — Easy tier gate
- Wilderness (KBD, God Wars Dungeon) — Medium tier; risk/reward unlock
- Prifddinas (Song of Elves) — Hard tier unlock; gear vendor access
- Desert (Beneath Cursed Sands) — Elite tier gate
- Raids (Fight Caves + Barrows gloves) — Hard tier gate; opens Chambers, Theatre
