# Zeah Locked Ironman — Progression Planner Extract

## Section/Phase Hierarchy

1. **Entry: Kourend Foothold**
   - Arrive from Tutorial Island; drop all pre-Zeah items
   - Establish camp in Land's End or Hosidius region
   - Minimum: all stats at 1, Combat 3, total level 34+, 1 quest point (Tutorial Island grants)

2. **Survival Foundation**
   - Gather raw food from local creatures (bear, beef, rat, chicken meat)
   - Establish basic melee gear progression (unarmed → Adamant → Rune)
   - Unlock respawn point (choice: Civitas via Colosseum or Kourend Castle via quests)

3. **Quest Unlocks Phase** (gated by survival baseline)
   - X Marks the Spot → thralls + Kharedst's memoirs teleport book
   - Druidic Ritual → unlock Herblore skill (enables potion production)
   - Eagles' Peak → box traps for Chinchompas (requires 27 Hunter)
   - Pandemonium → unlock Sailing skill + island access

4. **Early Skilling Interleave**
   - Hunter: Snare Crimson Swifts (1-9) then Copper Longtails (9-27), in parallel with combat food gathering
   - Herblore: Post-Druidic Ritual; pair potion training with Grubby Chest farming for supplies
   - Agility: Background Marks of Grace farming via Colossal Wyrm Course (trade 100 Termites → 1 Amylase pack)

5. **Respawn Point Acquisition**
   - **Civitas Route**: Win 5,000 Colosseum glory (~waves 6-7); speak Gloria near scoreboard
   - **Kourend Castle Route**: Complete A Kingdom Divided (requires prior: X Marks, Druidic Ritual exceptions)

6. **Varlamore Unlock**
   - Children of the Sun quest (no prerequisites listed; Kourend-only players skip)
   - Expands available content, gear tiers, and skilling resources

7. **Advanced Activities & Loops**
   - Grubby Chest farming (repeatable potions: Prayer, Super Restore, Saradomin Brews)
   - Courier Tasks (repeatable currency between Land's End and Hosidius port)
   - Slayer training via Konar or specialized Zeah methods
   - Colosseum progression toward higher waves (currency/supplies/access)

## Interleaving & Prep Wisdom

### Parallel Execution
- **Hunter + Combat**: Bird snaring happens while gathering raw meats from creatures in overlapping zones (Kebos creatures spawn near snaring areas). No wait time; alternate activities by zone.
- **Herblore + Grubby Chest**: Train Herblore via herb cleaning and potion brewing; Grubby Chest drops are just-in-time restocks, not a dedicated skill (save inventory).
- **Agility as Background**: Colossal Wyrm Amylase farming is repeatable and low-interaction; conduct between main activities.

### Time-Gated & Repeatable Chains
- **Grubby Chest** (Forthos Dungeon): Repeatable PvM drops of potions; no brewing cost, no herblore unlock required. Pull before Druidic Ritual if low on supplies.
- **Courier Tasks**: Repeatable income loop between Land's End and Hosidius port; runs in background on a cadence (check-in every 10-15 minutes).
- **Colosseum Glory**: Waves 6-7 yield ~2,000-2,500 glory per run; scale runs to accumulate 5,000 for respawn. Not mandatory to do all at once.

### Training Breakpoints & Ordering Rules
- **Hunter 27 breakpoint**: Box traps (Chinchompas, Jerboas) unlock after Eagles' Peak. Do not rush; snare Longtails until natural 27.
- **Herblore unlock gate**: Druidic Ritual is a one-time quest; all herbal ingredients (bear/beef/rat/chicken meat) are gathered simultaneously with early combat. Do Druidic Ritual early (low requirement) to open potion production.
- **Respawn choice ordering**: Civitas (Colosseum route) is less quest-intensive; prioritize if early respawn is desired. Kourend Castle requires quest chain accumulation (X Marks + Druidic Ritual + A Kingdom Divided), but enables A Kingdom Divided progre
ssion in parallel.

### "Do X While Y" Strategies
- **Snare while gathering meats**: Walk between meat-gathering creatures and bird-snaring spots in the same zone; toggle between activities per spawn clock.
- **Brew potions while farming Grubby**: After Druidic Ritual, mix herblore training with Grubby Chest runs; combine inventory (raw herbs + Grubby drops) to maximize per-trip value.
- **Agility course while waiting for respawn**: Colossal Wyrm course is low-attention; complete laps while Courier Tasks cooldown ticks.

### Ahead-of-Time vs Just-In-Time
- **Ahead-of-time**: Raw meats (hunt or kill in bulk before need), Agility marks (background farming before gear upgrades), Herbal materials (gather and clean stacks before training sessions).
- **Just-in-time**: Grubby Chest potions (pull as inventory allows), Courier Task payouts (check on demand for currency), Colosseum glory (run waves as respawn need arises).

## Self-Sufficiency & Gathering

### Food & Consumables
- **Raw Meats**: Killed from Hosidius/Kebos creatures (Cows, Bears, Giant Rats, Chickens). No GE dependence.
- **Potions (pre-Herblore)**: Grubby Chest in Forthos Dungeon drops Prayer potions, Super Restore, Saradomin Brews. Repeatable, no quest/level required.
- **Potions (post-Herblore)**: Brew from raw herbs (gathered on Zeah) + ashes/saltpetre/other ingredients (Zeah-based or dropped from Grubby).
- **Herbal Materials**: All raw herbs for Druidic Ritual (Bear meat, Beef, Rat meat, Chicken) sourced from Zeah creatures. Herb cleaning post-ritual prepares secondary ingredients.

### Gear & Armor Progression
- **Melee**: Start unarmed/wood weapons. Progress Adamant → Rune (smithed or looted from PvM). Oathplate/Blood moon are Zeah-exclusive endgame.
- **Ranged**: Arrow progression via fletching and archery/crossbow training. Atlatl darts (Zeah-exclusive). Hueycoatl hide (Zeah creatures → crafted armor).
- **Magic**: Blue wizard robes (looted or crafted); Xerician robes (Zeah skilling gear); Ancestral/Moon robes (Zeah PvM drops or high-level crafting).
- **Shields/Accessories**: Rune kiteshield (smithed), Oathplate variants (Zeah-exclusive), Moon/Eclipse variants (Zeah-exclusive).

### Marks of Grace & Amylase
- **Shayzien Course**: Natural Marks drop from agility training on Zeah. Low-attention repeatable.
- **Colossal Wyrm Course Bypass**: Trade 100 Termites for 1 Amylase pack at Worm Tongue's Wares. Termites sourced via cave farming or combat drops. Flexible just-in-time for marks.

### Group-Gathered Activities
- **Colosseum**: Solo PvM waves, but respawn progression (5,000 glory) is self-contained. No group requirement.
- **Courier Tasks**: Solo delivery loops. No group component.
- *(Guide does not detail group skilling or group-only production)*

## Steer-Point Orientation

### Milestones by Category

**Access Gates**
- Kourend entry (no prereq)
- Varlamore unlock via Children of the Sun quest
- Island access via Pandemonium quest + Sailing skill

**Respawn Points** (non-level, critical infrastructure)
- Civitas illa Fortis: 5,000 Colosseum glory (repeatable, self-paced)
- Kourend Castle: A Kingdom Divided quest (requires X Marks + Druidic Ritual exceptions)

**Quest Unlocks** (gating key activities)
- X Marks the Spot → Thralls + Teleport book
- Druidic Ritual → Herblore skill + potion production
- Eagles' Peak → Box traps (requires 27 Hunter)
- Pandemonium → Sailing skill + island travel

**Skill Thresholds** (non-level progression gates)
- Hunter 27 → Box traps (Chinchompas, Jerboas)
- Herblore 3+ → Potion brewing (Druidic Ritual required first)
- Sailing 5+ → Initial island access (Pandemonium grants level 5)

**Collection/Gear Tiers** (non-quest progression)
- Adamant armor → Rune → Dragon → Oathplate/Blood moon
- Arrow progression → Rune arrows → Dragon arrows → Atlatl darts
- Basic robes → Xerician → Ancestral/Moon variants
- Agility marks accumulation (no hard cap; repeatable background)

**Repeatable Loops & Cadences**
- Grubby Chest farming (potion resupply, continuous)
- Colosseum glory farming (respawn progression, self-paced to 5,000)
- Courier Tasks (currency generation, check-in cadence ~10-15 min)

### Steer-Point Sequencing Rules
1. **Respawn first**: Establish respawn (Civitas or Kourend) within first few hours to unlock death safety.
2. **Druidic Ritual early**: Unlock Herblore as soon as survivable to open potion production and pair with food gathering.
3. **Hunter 27 before Eagles' Peak**: Reach 27 via snaring, then quest, then unlock box traps. Do not quest before level.
4. **Varlamore defer or immediate**: Children of the Sun is unlock-only; content is optional. Defer if early-game respawn/quest priorities dominate.
5. **Agility marks as background**: Never a blocker; farm continuously in parallel with primary activities.
