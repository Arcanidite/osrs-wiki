# Mootrius Ironman Guide — Structure & Requisites

## Section/Phase Hierarchy

1. **Getting Started** (Foundation Systems — must complete before general combat)
   - Thieving to Rogue's Outfit (60 Thieving via progression: men→bakers→fruit stalls→blackjacking→wealthy)
   - Prayer via Hunter Rumours (75 Hunter unlocks moonlight moth mixtures; baseline 30 Prayer via big bones)
   - Food via Tempoross (65 Fishing unlocks karambwan production; fast recovery during combat)

2. **Combat Foundations** (Parallel quest tracks)
   - Melee quests: Fight Arena, Tree Gnome Village, Grand Tree, Merlin's Crystal, Holy Grail, Dragon Slayer I, Monkey Madness I
   - Ranged quests: Goblin Diplomacy, Lost Tribe, Death to Dorgeshuun, Demon Slayer, Shadow of Storm, Contact!

3. **Combat Gear & Stats** (Target: 70 Att/Str/Def, 66 Ranged, 75 Magic)
   - Helmet progression via quests (berserker, archer, farseer, neitiznot)
   - Amulets from H.A.M. Storerooms (strength, power; enchanted)
   - Magic 60 via blue dragon safespotting; 75 via additional training
   - Dragon defender from Warriors' Guild
   - Slayer 58 minimum as primary combat delivery method
   - Mixed hide + mystic robes
   - Mage Training Arena unlocks (bones to peaches, rune pouch, infinity boots)

4. **Starting Farming** (Herb production gate → scalable supply line)
   - Farming quests: Giant Dwarf, Forgettable Tale, Creature of Fenkenstrain, Garden of Tranquillity, Garden of Death → 35 Farming
   - Tithe Farm to 50 Farming; 300 reward points for auto-weed + herb sack unlock (58 Herblore prerequisite)
   - Master farmer pickpocketing for seeds; farming contracts for diversity
   - Pineapple composting for supercompost
   - Herb patches as foundation; trees/other patches expand later

5. **Hunters' Sunlight Crossbow** (Mid-tier ranged weapon via hybrid gathering + production)
   - Fletching quests: X Marks the Spot, Client of Kourend, Tourist Trap → baseline XP
   - Vale Totems minigame to 74 Fletching
   - Log gathering: 170 oak, 500 willow, 4,020 maple via forestry
   - Sunlight antelope hunting (72 Hunter prerequisite met earlier)
   - Crossbow + bolts fletching

6. **Morytania Hard Diary** (Barrows gate; extensive but sequenced carefully)
   - Skills: 71 Agility (Colossal Wyrm course), 70 Prayer, 55 Mining, 50 Smithing, 50 Construction, 45 Crafting, 50 Firemaking
   - Quests: Death Plateau, Troll Stronghold, Temple of Ikov, Desert Treasure I, multiple Recipe for Disaster subs (sequenced for prerequisites)
   - Reward: 50% bonus runes from Barrows; Morytania legs 3 teleport

7. **Quick Detour — Passive Resources** (Before Barrows grind)
   - Bone Voyage quest + Kudos accumulation unlock Fossil Island
   - Birdhouses (levels 5–90 progressively) → bird nests for potion supplies (background activity)
   - Giant seaweed farming on Fossil Island (preparation for superglass make phase)

8. **Barrows** (Primary sustained grind)

## Interleaving & Prep Wisdom (Core Philosophy)

**Alternation discipline:**
- Explicit design: alternate active grinds (thieving, slayer combat) with AFK/background (birdhouses, farm runs, seaweed plots)
- Quote: "break up your more active grinds with some AFK ones" + "read ahead to find alternation opportunities"
- Prevents burnout; guides recommend deviating "in any way you see fit" for player enjoyment

**Dependency layering:**
- Three foundational systems (money→Rogue's outfit, prayer→Hunter, food→Karambwan) must run first; unlock all downstream combat efficiency
- Farming setup sequenced so quests feed Kudos → Fossil Island unlock (passive resources)
- Morytania diary deliberately follows farming setup; prior quest completions provide required Kudos accumulation

**JIT vs. ahead-of-time:**
- **Ahead-of-time:** Birdhouses, seaweed plots, herb patches run continuously in background during active grinding (fire-and-forget passive)
- **JIT:** Herb runs expand later when other content is in flight; initially "not terribly important"
- **Batching:** Farming quests completed as cluster before Tithe Farm (prerequisite unlocking)

**Breakpoint + movement rules:**
- Combat training targets set (70/70/70 Att/Str/Def, 66 Ranged, 75 Magic) before advancing to Barrows
- Slayer used as primary combat delivery (multitask: train stats + gather drops)
- Fletching 74 needed before crossbow; requires Vale Totems minigame participation (no pure skilling alternative)
- Agility 71 (Colossal Wyrm course) gates Morytania diary; must breakpoint before diary grind

**Ordering heuristics:**
- Solve supply problems (money, prayer, food) BEFORE combat training scales demand
- Complete prerequisite quest chains (melee → ranged) in parallel, not sequentially
- H.A.M. thieving unlocks after Rogue's outfit; doubled loot efficiency step-up gates amulet acquisition
- Fossil Island passive resources initialized mid-run, not at start (Kudos dependency)

- 72 Hunter (sunlight antelope hunting prerequisite)
- 74 Fletching (crossbow production gate)
