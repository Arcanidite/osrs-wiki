# Ironman Multiquest Guide — Structured Extract

## Section/Phase Hierarchy & Checkpoints

1. **Getting Started** (Lumbridge)
   - Tutorial Island, initiate 4 quests (Rune Mysteries, X Marks the Spot, Restless Ghost, Misthalin Mystery)
   - Gather starter items (jug, knife, leather gloves)

2. **Early GP Gen** (Bounty Hunter → Al Kharid)
   - Die at Bounty Hunter, reclaim steel platelegs in Lumbridge
   - Sell platelegs to Louie Legs: 14.7k GP (world-hop)
   - Gate: ~15k GP to fund initial quest item purchases

3. **Port Sarim Hub** (Shantay Pass → Port Sarim)
   - Buy: 5 ropes, 10 bronze bars, water bowls, hammer, feather, buckets
   - Complete Pandemonium, Witch's Potion
   - Gather: giant rat meat, ashes, onion, snape grass
   - Resource: Betty (black wizard hat, eye of newt, runes, air/water runes) — 12.6k GP

4. **Ardougne Cluster** (Ship → Ardougne)
   - Execute 6 quests sequentially: Sheep Herder, Monk's Friend, Clock Tower, Hazeel Cult, Tree Gnome Village (+ grizzly bear kill), Fight Arena
   - Purchases at Gnome Stronghold: dwellberries, onions, flour, milk, steel axe
   - Gate: Spirit tree unlocked (access to Gnome fast-travel)

5. **Plague & Mining** (Ardougne → Rimmington → Falador)
   - Complete Plague City, progress Biohazard
   - Mine: 16 clay, 4 copper, 2 iron ore for crafting
   - Checkpoint: Basic ores stockpiled for later furnace/anvil work

6. **Falador Hub & Gear Tier 1** (PvP world banking)
   - Start Knight's Sword, Black Knights' Fortress
   - Bank run at PvP world: bulk-buy 14 steel/iron battleaxes (2k GP, hopped)
   - Checkpoint: Adamant sword + anti-dragon shield progression unlocked

7. **Mid-Quest Weave** (Varrock teleport via Chronicle)
   - 9 quests progress/complete in Varrock hub: Romeo & Juliet, Daddy's Home, Gertrude's Cat, Shield of Arrav, Children of the Sun, Demon Slayer, Priest in Peril, Family Crest, Rune Mysteries
   - Purchases: pink skirt, priest robes, chisel, iron chainbody, adamant sword, 90 steel nails
   - Side-activity: Make 2 redberry pies while traveling (pick 4 redberries)
   - Gate: Client of Kourend route opens (minecart fast-travel system)

8. **Draynor Eastern** (Walking routes, lever teleports)
   - Ides of Milk progress, Ernest the Chicken, Vampyre Slayer complete, A Porcine of Interest steps
   - Dye crafting: blue, red, yellow dyes for Goblin Diplomacy
   - Activity: Dig behind Draynor Manor (FTP1 skull for X Marks)
   - Checkpoint: Sorceress's Garden unlocked (repeatable teleport access)

9. **Minigame Teleport Cascade** (Soul Wars, Bounty Hunter, NMZ, Ferox, Sorceress's Garden, Giant's Foundry)
   - Complete: Druidic Ritual (unlock Herblore), Doric's Quest, Witch's House, Fishing Contest, Recruitment Drive
   - Advanced locks: Into the Abyss (mine 24 essence, obtain silverlight keys)
   - Gate: Herblore unlocked; minigame network fully mapped for JIT routing

10. **Advanced Quest Chain** (Multi-region routing via spirit trees, minigames, levers)
    - Sleeping Giants: craft 2 swords at Giant's Foundry (24k GP cost)
    - Sheep Herder completion (kill all sheep at pen)
    - Dwarf Cannon + Waterfall Quest interleave: pebble fetch via spirit tree
    - The Grand Tree progression (water strike required, final step)
    - Dragon Slayer I: multi-phase route (Oracle → Essence mining → Silverlight fetch → Boat repair @ 33 Magic → Kill Elvarg)
    - Ides of Milk completion (requires ~1k Prayer XP)
    - Final checkpoint: Anti-dragon shield unlocked, core access to Morytania implied (guide incomplete at this point)

## Interleaving & Prep Wisdom (CRITICAL)

### JIT Resource Strategy
- **Purchasing is pipelined ahead of region**: Before traveling to Ardougne, buy Shantay supplies once; before Varrock, buy all Varrock-hub items in one bank run
- **No ahead-of-time stockpiling**: Supplies are bought and used immediately within a phase; minimal carried inventory
- **Banking at checkpoints**: Ferox lever, minigame teleports, and PvP world banking used to dump/withdraw selectively by region

### Simultaneous Quest Progression
- **4–9 quests initiated/progressed in parallel**: Each region hub (Ardougne, Varrock, Draynor) has multiple quests running concurrently
- **Ordering rule**: Quests that share NPCs or locations complete together to minimize walking
- **Gate pattern**: Some quests progress until a skill gate or item gate (e.g., Biohazard stops until mould from Prince Ali), then resume later

### Skill Leveling Embedded in Quest Flow
- **Herblore**: Unlocked mid-guide via Druidic Ritual (quest gate, not pre-trained)
- **Prayer**: Farmed to ~1k XP via bones gathered/looted during earlier quest phases; checked at Ides of Milk (hard gate)
- **Magic**: 33 Magic required for boat repair in Dragon Slayer I; assumed trained passively via spell-casting in quests
- **Fletching**: 10 levels for Tourist Trap (casual training expected between phases)
- **Agility**: Gained as side-effect during Tourist Trap completion
- **Mining**: Episodic (16 clay, 4 copper, 2 iron harvested in Phase 5 dedicated block; no ongoing runs)

### Do-While Pattern
- **Travel interleaving**: Make dyes for Goblin Diplomacy while routing to other locations
- **Gear waiting**: Complete X Marks (dig → dig → dig) while Pirate's Treasure awaits Varrock-hub key
- **Quest continuation vs. restart**: Some quests (Biohazard, Knight's Sword, Dragon Slayer I) progress in 3–5 stages across 10+ other quest completions; resumption is explicit per phase

### Background Activity Absence
- **No farm runs, birdhouse, or production cadences** mentioned
- **No pre-prep gathering** (e.g., "chop 50 logs now, use in 10 quests")
- Implies guide is SHORT-DURATION or assumes skills are trained elsewhere; guide is multiquest-focused, not grinding-focused

### Supply Chain Assumption
- **Items are either bought or killed for**; no item-making loops (except dyes, redberry pies, swords at foundry which are explicit in phase)
- **No flipping or grand-exchange arbitrage** (ironman constraint, implicit)
- **Coin efficiency**: World-hopping to Louie Legs and bulk-buying at PvP world treated as optimization tactics
