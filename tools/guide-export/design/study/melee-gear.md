# Melee Gear Progression

## Section/Phase Hierarchy

### F2P Metal Tiers (Levels 1–40)
Iron → Steel → Mithril → Adamant → Rune. Sequential unlocks by level; full kit cost under 120k gp.

### Early Members: Quest Checkpoint (Levels 40–60)
**Recipe for Disaster gate**: Bridges from Rune to Barrows-capable gear. Unlocks Barrows gloves (eternal BiS for hands slot).
- Pre-RfD: Rune + Ardougne cloak + Combat bracelet (135k gp).
- Post-RfD: Adds Berserker helm, Dragon scimitar, Fighter torso progression.

### Mid Members: Defender & Whip Grind (Levels 60–85)
Dragon defender (60 Attack unlock at Warriors' Guild). Abyssal whip threshold (85 Slayer, ~7.7M gp). Helm of Neitiznot (60 Defence). Staggered boss/Slayer runs begin.

### Late Game: Specialized Stab/Crush/Slash (Levels 75–95)
Abyssal tentacle (Kraken, 11.7M), Serpentine helm (charged via Zulrah scales, 116k/hour drain). Amulet of torture (20M+). Neitiznot faceguard (95 Defence). Branches into weapon-class specialization.

### End Game: Torva / Scythe Era (Levels 90–99)
Blade of saeldor (727M), Inquisitor's mace (1.05B), Ghrazi rapier (975M), Scythe of vitur (2.23B). Each represents final-tier specialization; diminishing returns on marginal damage.

## Task Grammar

Example tasks (4–6 short paraphrased actions):
1. "Complete Recipe for Disaster; obtain Barrows gloves."
2. "Train Slayer to 85; grind Abyssal whip task."
3. "Farm Zulrah or Kraken; charge Serpentine helm upkeep."
4. "Unlock Dragon defender via Warriors' Guild; equip at 60 Attack."
5. "Grind Barrows armor drops; assemble slash/crush/stab loadout."
6. "Accumulate 11.7M gp; buy Abyssal tentacle for late-game transition."

## Interleaving & Prep Wisdom

**Parallel specialization tracks**: The guide encodes three orthogonal weapon-class progressions (Slash via Saeldor, Crush via Inquisitor, Stab via Rapier). Player selects content type (bossing, slayer, raids) and rides that track, minimizing redundant gear purchases.

**Quest-gated checkpoint**: Recipe for Disaster is a hard prerequisite for mid-level efficiency; Barrows gloves unlock and remain BiS for gauntlets indefinitely. This serializes early-to-mid transition.

**Slayer as backbone**: 85 Slayer is the stepping stone to Abyssal whip, which is the first "big jump" in damage (~16 bonus). Slayer runs BATCH consumables (drops) and gear (Barrows) just-in-time.

**Charge-drain cycles**: Serpentine helm requires Zulrah scales (116k gp/hour upkeep via farming Zulrah). This ties gear maintenance to **background Zulrah runs** on a cadence—not a one-time acquisition.

**Fire cape as free milestone**: TzHaar content yields Infernal cape (0 gp cost) at high-level; represents a steer point for PvM progression without cash sink.

**Wilderness ether**: Revenant ether (Wilderness, 264k gp/hour) charges Ursine chainmace. This requires player to run Wilderness content in prep blocks, balancing risk/reward and supply chaining.

**Boss farming + Slayer interleaving**: High-level melee (80+) transitions into parallel boss farming (Tombs, Kraken, Zulrah) while Slayer continues; both drop gear and resources (scales, shards, drops).

## Self-Sufficiency / Gathering

**Melee weapons via bossing / Slayer**:
- Fighter torso: Barbarian Assault minigame (0 cost, time-bound).
- Dragon defender: Warriors' Guild, 60 Attack gate (free).
- Fire cape / Infernal cape: TzHaar combat (consumable food only, 0 item cost).
- Abyssal whip: Slayer tasks (drops from Slayer creatures).
- Ghrazi rapier: Tombs of Amascut boss farming.
- Scythe of vitur: Raids farming.
- Barrows equipment: Barrows dungeon minigame.

**Armour via Slayer & Bossing**:
- Rune, Adamant, Mithril tiers: Bought initially (F2P-era progression), but full-plate armor is durable and replaced infrequently.
- Berserker helm: No direct source noted (imply GE, flag for unwinding).
- Helm of Neitiznot: No direct quest/farm source (flag coarse).
- Serpentine helm: Zulrah drops, requires scales (116k/hour upkeep via farming Zulrah).
- Barrows gloves: RfD completion (no gp cost once quest done).
- Torva plate armor, Neitiznot faceguard, Ultor ring, Bellator ring: Raid / boss-specific drops (Torva is CoX, others are GWD2/T4 raids, flag coarse farming routes).

**Consumables**:
- Food: Fishing (self-source) or purchased (Gauntlet supplies, cooking upkeep).
- Potions: Herblore + Farming upkeep (farm herbs just-in-time or ahead-of-time in batches).
- Runes: Runecrafting or purchased (assume prepared JIT).
- Zulrah scales: Zulrah drops (recurring background run, 116k/hour drain for Serpentine charge).
- Ancient shards: Unknown direct source (flag coarse).
- Revenant ether: Wilderness drops (flag coarse farming conditions).

**Missing direct sources** (flag for downstream unwinding): Berserker helm, Neitiznot helm, Torva armor, Scythe, Ghrazi rapier source details, Ultor/Bellator rings, Ancient shards, full Revenant ether farming breakdown.

## Steer-Point Orientation

**Non-level milestones** (steering hooks):

1. **Recipe for Disaster completion**: Unlocks Barrows gloves (eternal gauntlet slot anchor). Hard prerequisite before mid-game scaling.
2. **Dragon defender unlock** (60 Attack prerequisite): Marks shift from 2h weapon to defender + 1h weapon offhand; damage scaling inflection.
3. **Abyssal whip acquisition** (85 Slayer prerequisite): First major damage spike (+16 strength bonus); psychological breakpoint for melee training.
4. **Infernal cape achievement**: 0-cost, high-PvM-rep milestone; marks completion of TzHaar difficulty tier.
5. **Torva/Scythe thresholds**: Final-tier specializations; represent content-specific (Raids, CoX) lock-in decisions.
6. **Serpentine helm charge cycle**: Ongoing resource drain (Zulrah scales); introduces maintenance-based recurring content.

**Level milestones that steer**:
- 40 Attack: Baseline for members entry (Rune weapons).
- 60 Attack: Dragon defender unlock.
- 70 Defence: Broad armour unlock (most Barrows + newer gear requires 60–70).
- 85 Slayer: Abyssal whip + wider Slayer task pool.
- 90+ Attack/Defence: End-game specialization entry.

**Collection log / gear-count milestones** (implied): Barrows full set (14 pieces); Torva full set (3 pieces); Raid drops (Ghrazi, Scythe, Ultor). These are progression "gates" but not explicitly framed in the guide.

**Access gates**:
- Barbarian Assault (Fighter torso): Unknown req (flag coarse).
- Warriors' Guild (Dragon defender): 60 Attack + gate entry mechanism.
- TzHaar (Fire/Infernal cape): Combat-based; no level gate stated but difficulty-gated.
- Barrows (armour drops): Access mechanism not specified (flag coarse).
- Zulrah (Serpentine + scales): Access mechanism not specified (flag coarse).
- Tombs of Amascut (Ghrazi): Requirements not specified (flag coarse).
- Raids (Scythe, Torva, Ultor): Requirements not specified (flag coarse).
- Revenant cave (Ether for Ursine): Wilderness access; anti-PK risk (not explicitly noted in guide).
