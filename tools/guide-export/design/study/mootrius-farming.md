# Mootrius Ironman Farming Guide — Burndown Structure

## Section/Phase Hierarchy

### Phase 1: Quest-Accelerated Leveling (1–35)
Checkpoint: Reach level 35 via four specific quests rather than crop grinding.
- Fairytale I - Growing Pains (req: Lost City, Nature Spirit; grants 1→17)
- Forgettable Tale (req: Cooking 22; grants 17→25)
- Garden of Tranquillity (req: multiple prerequs; grants 25→30)
- Garden of Death (no skill req; grants 30→35)

**Estimated runtime:** few hours total.

### Phase 2: Foundation & Tools (Parallel to/after Level 35)
Checkpoint: Tooling complete, compost pipeline established.
- Acquire farming tools (magic secateurs, rake, seed dibber, spade, trowel)
- Establish watering can supply (multiple recommended)
- Set up compost system (ultracompost mandatory before herb cycling)
- Populate filled plant pots in bulk

### Phase 3: Active Herb Farming Cycle
Checkpoint: Sustained herb runs on ~90-minute cadence.
- Weekly/ongoing: Slayer training (passive seed feed)
- Pre-cycle: Volcanic ash mining (Fossil Island, tied to compost top-up)
- Cycle: Plant herbs at patches, harvest 90min later, replant

## Task Grammar

**One task format:** `[location patch] plant/tend/harvest [herb name], [action detail], [benefit]`

**Example tasks (4-6, ≤12 words each):**
1. Plant ranarr in Ardougne patch with ultracompost; high yield.
2. Do slayer task; collect herb seed drops passively.
3. Mine volcanic ash at Fossil Island; replenish compost bin.
4. Harvest herbs from five patches, bank them into supply.
5. Complete Bone Voyage quest; unlock Fossil Island ash access.
6. Use magic secateurs when harvesting; +10% yield protection.

## Interleaving & Prep Wisdom (Core to Burndown Model)

### Slayer as Seed Pipeline
- **Passive mechanism:** Herb seeds drop from all Slayer monsters (Dagannoth through Torstol).
- **Seed availability:** Ranges from Common (Guam, Marrentill) to Rare (Torstol).
- **Ordering rule:** Slayer training FEEDS farming — prioritize monsters matching your farming level's seed tier; don't delay Slayer for farming, farm feeds Slayer.

### Compost & Ash Synchronization
- **Ultracompost prerequisite:** Non-negotiable before herb runs scale; requires Bone Voyage (Fossil Island unlock).
- **Just-in-time ash:** Mine volcanic ash *during* compost decay (~90min cycle boundary) — parallelizable with herb patch idle time.
- **Prep ahead:** Collect pineapples from Catherby (charter crewmember, non-GE) before compost bins full.

### 90-Minute Herb Cycle Cadence
- **Pattern:** Plant at all patches → 90min wait → Harvest, replant.
- **Parallel activities during wait:** Slayer, quest progression, Herblore training (use farmed herbs).
- **Repetition:** Enables continuous herb supply for Herblore training (a later unlock driver).

### Quest-Unlock Sequencing
- **Fairytale I** and earlier perquisites unlock first before Level 30.
- **Bone Voyage** must land before ultracompost production (gates Fossil Island ash access).
- **Do X while Y:** Slayer runs WHILE waiting for farm cycle; herb harvests feed Herblore training.

### Tool Acquisition Timing
- **Magic secateurs:** Yield boost (+10% seed retention); buy early.
- **Watering cans:** Plural supply (multiple filled) before scaling runs.
- **Seed dibber, rake, spade, trowel:** Batch purchase; "a few thousand coins" total cost.

## Self-Sufficiency / Gathering

### Herb Seeds (Primary Supply)
- **Source:** PvM/Slayer monsters exclusively (no GE buying framing).
- **Tier-locked acquisition:** Dagannoth (Slayer 1) → Aberrant Spectre (60) → Kurask (70) → Nechryael (80) → top-tier herbs.
- **Activity:** Integrate into main Slayer progression; seeds are a *byproduct* of skill leveling.

### Volcanic Ash (Compost Ingredient)
- **Source:** Fossil Island ash piles (mined after Bone Voyage unlock).
- **Gathering:** Inline with patch cycle idle time; no separate trip.

### Pineapples (Compost Input)
- **Source:** Charter crewmembers at Catherby docks (non-GE NPC trade).
- **Quantity:** Bulk 30-unit runs per compost bin cycle.
- **Timing:** Pre-stockpile before compost bins begin.

### Buckets (Compost Storage)
- **Source:** Farming shops (purchase empty buckets, fill via compost bins).
- **Logistics:** Maintain enough filled buckets for multi-cycle herb supply.

## Steer-Point Orientation (Milestones & Unlocks)

### Level Checkpoints
- **Level 17** (Fairytale I complete) — baseline farming capability.
- **Level 25** (Forgettable Tale complete) — mid-tier unlocks.
- **Level 30** (Garden of Tranquillity complete).
- **Level 35** (Garden of Death complete) — free of early quest grinding.

### Quest-Gated Access
- **Fairytale I, Forgettable Tale, Garden of Tranquillity, Garden of Death** — these are NON-LEVEL steer-points; cannot skip.
- **Bone Voyage** — unlocks Fossil Island for ash; mandatory for scaled compost.

### Gear/Tool Acquisition Milestone
- **Magic secateurs** (yield boost).
- **Watering can suite** (reliability at scale).
- **Seed dibber, rake, spade, trowel** (standard ops).

### Infrastructure Steer-Point
- **Ultracompost pipeline online** — transition from low-yield to sustainable herb cycling.

### Activity-Gated Milestone
- **Herb farming sustainable** — Slayer feeding seeds; Herblore consuming herbs; compost replenishing on cycle.
