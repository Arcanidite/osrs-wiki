## Section/Phase Hierarchy

### Account Setup (Gate: Tutorial Island → Stronghold → Christmas Event)
- Complete Tutorial Island
- Stronghold of Security (free 10k Attack XP, Fighting boots unlock, Skull sceptre unlock)
- Christmas Event (one-time snow globe pickup for tick-manipulation methods)

### Quest Completion Phase (Prerequisite for armor/levels)
- **Buyables** (minimal gp): Doric's Quest, Sheep Shearer, Cook's Assistant, Imp Catcher, Goblin Diplomacy
- **Quick/Easy** (no requirements): Prince Ali, Restless Ghost, Rune Mysteries, Witch's Potion, Romeo & Juliet
- **Combat quests** (20-40 Def gating): Vampyre Slayer, Knight's Sword, Corsair Curse, Below Ice Mountain, Demon Slayer, Black Knights' Fortress, Dragon Slayer I
- **Checkpoint**: Dragon Slayer I unlocks rune god armor + green d'hide (core gear gates)

### Tier 1: Fast Buyable Skills (First 20-40 hours)
- **Cooking** (1-99): jugs of wine breakpoint at 35; never-burn threshold 86
- **Firemaking** (1-99): yew logs phase at 60; enables teleport loop (passive Magic gain via Varrock TP)
- **Smithing** (29-99): platebody chain; profitability pivot at 88 (adamant alchemy break-even)

### Tier 2: Concurrent Mid-Layer Skills (Parallel to Buyables, 80-150 hours each)
- **Woodcutting** (1-99): AFK Forestry phase then 2-tick oaks acceleration at 60
- **Fishing** (1-99): Free pickup method (Barbarian Village) then 3-tick acceleration with snow globe
- **Runecraft** (1-99): Passive/low-priority; layered into downtime

### Tier 3: Resource-Heavy Milestone (Combine with Combat)
- **Prayer** (9-99): Scatter-bury/power-bury or zero-time burial during hill/moss giant combat

### Tier 4: Combat Interleave (150-200 hours, multiskilled with Prayer + Magic)
- **Melee** (1-99): Monks → flesh crawlers AFK → hill/moss giants (key drops + prayer bones during combat)
- **Ranged** (1-99): Parallel safespot training at same giants
- **Magic** (1-99): AFK splash phase → High Level Alchemy passive → concurrent alchemy during Smithing/combat

### Tier 5: Endgame Verification & Loot Loop
- **Beginner Clue Scrolls**: Hill giant + clue juggling (collection log verification of F2P-pure status)

## Interleaving & Prep Wisdom

### Zero-Time XP Layering
- **Alchemy + Smithing**: Alternate alchemy casts on item returns to bank during platebody smithing. Covers Smithing gold cost at 88+ Smithing; 2k+ Magic XP/hr passive.
- **Alchemy + Combat**: Cast High Level Alchemy on enemy drops/loot during downtime between auto-attacks or bank returns. Sustained 1k-2k Magic XP/hr passive.
- **Prayer + Melee**: Bury bones between auto-attacks at hill/moss giants. Full Prayer 99 absorption during concurrent Melee training; avoids dedicated grind.
- **Varrock Teleport (Magic 25) + Firemaking**: Used in yew log loop. Passive 2k+ Magic XP/hr during active Firemaking.

### Background Activity Cadence (Ahead-of-Time vs. Just-in-Time)
- **Woodcutting (AFK Forestry)**: Run continuously in background across weeks of main training. Zero inventory impact; drop logs. Scales to 100% oak success at 60+ (2-tick method ceiling).
- **Fishing (pickup method)**: Run during short breaks or concurrent with other inventory-based skills. Barbarian Village trout/salmon free-to-cook sustains food costs. Low attention; 30-40k XP/hr passive.
- **Magic splash (AFK)**: Run 20-minute attention windows at Stronghold level 2 or lesser demons. Reach 25 Magic early (teleport unlock); reach 55 Magic before intensive gold sinks (alchemy break-even).

### Skill Batching & Ordering Logic
- **Complete all buyable quests first** (instant XP thresholds + free armor gates). Minimal gp cost; unlocks best-in-slot gear.
- **Cooking 1-35 early** (enable wine phase 35-99). High gp/hr efficiency unlocks Firemaking + Smithing funding.
- **Firemaking 1-60 mid** (parallel with Cooking end/Smithing start). Yew phase enables teleport loop (passive Magic).
- **Magic 25-55 AFK during firemaking/farming downtime**. Teleports reduce movement friction; alchemy break-even at 55.
- **Smithing 29-99 as gp permits** (interleave with Fishing/Woodcutting low-attention phases). Alchemy passive covers cost at 88+.
- **Melee 1-99 + Prayer burial concurrent**. Hill/moss giants provide keys + prayer bones; zero-time burial during combat.
- **Ranged 1-99 as secondary combat layer** (safespot giants simultaneous with Melee or Fishing overflow).

### Multiskilling by Location (Single-Site Efficiency)
- **Barbarian Village**: Fly-fish trout → drop → cook on nearby fire in inventory gaps. Sustains food; layers Fishing + Cooking + Strength-based Melee (barbarian training).
- **Edgeville Dungeon**: Hill giants (Melee + key drops + prayer bones) → beginner clues (Ranged safespot + loot) → Obor boss door (skull sceptre teleport reset). Consolidates combat training + item loops.
- **Stronghold of Security**: Flesh crawlers AFK (Melee 20-85) → giant spiders (active Melee 50+) → level 2 splash (Magic AFK). Stacking efficient for single-site training.
- **Falador smithy / West Varrock**: Smithing anvil + teleport loop (Firemaking passive). Bank tags (Menu Entry Swapper) + ESC key close enable preset-bar alignment for 300+ XP/click.

### Tick Manipulation Phases (High-Focus Acceleration)
- **2-Tick Oaks (Woodcutting 60-99)**: Wind strike rat splashes + drag into multicombat zone → click oak on hitsplat tick. 100k XP/hr sustained. Replicable 5-min setup; scales with Rune axe (60 WC perfect rate).
- **3-Tick Fly-Fishing (Fishing 20-99, requires snow globe)**: Collect snow (2 ticks) → fish (3 total). Snow Tracker plugin ensures respawn at 6-tick mark. Drop during transition if snow ≥6 remaining. Medium focus; 61k XP/hr at 78 Fishing.
- **Scatter-Bury Prayer (during combat)**: Alternate scatter/bury every tick with vile ashes + big bones. 100k XP/hr sustained but requires consistent clicking. Absorbable into Melee if paced correctly.

## Self-Sufficiency & Gathering Strategy

### F2P Consumables (No Grand Exchange — In-Game Sourcing Only)

**Food:**
- Fly-fishing pickup (Barbarian Village trout/salmon): Free; sustains Melee training + low Cooking requirement enables cooking on-site.
- Cooking on fire: Raw trout/salmon + water vessel (low gp/unit). Scales to wine-burning (no food; pure gp cost).

**Runes (Magic):**
- All runes MUST be purchased (no in-game production efficient in F2P).
- Body runes (Runecraft 20+): Slow production; purchasing recommended.
- Runecraft tiara link (Crafting required): Enables +50% Runecraft speed but tied to Crafting bottleneck.

**Arrows (Ranged):**
- MUST be purchased (no fletcher availability in F2P).
- Adamant arrows (ranged cost): -34 gp/shot sustained; -68k gp/hr at high intensity.

**Bones/Prayer Supplies:**
- Hill/moss giant drops during Melee training: Combine Prayer burial with combat auto-attacks (zero-time bury).
- Vile ashes (purchased): Scatter-bury method primary cost (~125M gp for full Prayer 9-99 scatter-bury).
- Alternative: Power-bury alone (521k XP/hr lower cost, 181+ hours dedicated).

**Logs (Woodcutting → Firemaking/Cooking):**
- Cut vs. purchase trade-off. Forestry AFK yields free logs; yew logs for fire cost 114 gp/ea but faster than cutting (time value).
- Early phase: Burn free logs (oak → willow → maple) to reach 60. Late phase: Purchase yew (speed over cost).

**Smithed Items (Platebodies, bars):**
- Must mine or purchase bars (no F2P ore production efficient vs. purchasing).
- Cost scales with bar tier (bronze to adamant); offset via alchemy passive at 88+ Smithing.

### Group Activities (None explicitly required for solo F2P)
- Shield of Arrav quest: REQUIRES second player cooperation (noted but avoidable as buyable quest).

### Clue Scroll Loop (Verification + Secondary Loot)
- Drop beginner clues at bank entrance.
- Juggle reorder via bank interface (maintain multiple active clues simultaneously).
- Rotate spawns: Edgeville Dungeon goblins, other goblin patches.
- Reward: Collection log entries (music, loot) verify F2P-pure status (no built-in F2P tracker).

## Key Interleaving Wisdom & Ordering Rules

1. **Complete all buyable quests before intensive training.** Instant XP thresholds + free gear gates unlock downstream efficiency.
2. **Reach Magic 25 early.** Teleports reduce movement cost across entire playthrough by 40-60% (time value compounding).
3. **Cooking 1-35 within first 3-4 hours.** Unlocks wine phase (480k XP/hr at 68+); gp/hr efficiency funds Prayer/Smithing/Firemaking.
4. **Firemaking mid-playthrough; layer Magic splash during downtime.** Enables yew phase (200k+ XP/hr) + Varrock teleport acceleration (passive Magic 2-3k/hr).
5. **Smithing 29-99 asynchronously with Fishing/Woodcutting low-attention phases.** Alchemy passive covers cost at 88+; never net-negative after that.
6. **Melee + Prayer burial concurrent.** Zero-time burial strategy absorbs Prayer 99 into Melee training; avoids 100+ hour dedicated grind.
7. **Ranged as secondary layer at same combat spawns.** Safespot same giants (no damage taken = AFK parallel training).
8. **Tick manipulation (2-tick oaks, 3-tick fishing, scatter-bury) optional acceleration phases.** High focus but 20-50% XP/hr gains; can be deferred to late-game or skipped for relaxed play.
9. **Beginner clue loop (hill giants + juggling) as endgame verification.** Collection log entries replace built-in F2P tracker; low-attention once combat foundation laid.

## Summary: Burndown-Ready Requisites Model

Xerxes' F2P guides a **unified F2P→future-P2P continuum** emphasizing:
- **Prerequisite resolution via gathering/production** (zero Grand Exchange buyables).
- **Interleaving efficiency**: Skills layered by attention cost (high/medium/low phases rotate).
- **Zero-time XP absorption**: Alchemy, Prayer burial, teleport loops embed secondary gains into unavoidable main-skill downtime.
- **Skill ordering matters more than skill leveling**. Early gate access (Magic 25, Cooking 35) unlocks downstream acceleration; gear-tier gates (Dragon Slayer I) force early combat investment but pay dividends in all melee-family skills.
- **Steer points are mixed**: Levels (60 Woodcutting, 43 Prayer), quests (Dragon Slayer I), gear tiers (rune god, green d'hide), and activity gates (clue juggling, 2-tick oaks) equally define progression.
