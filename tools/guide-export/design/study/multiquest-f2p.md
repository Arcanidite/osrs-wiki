# Free-to-play Multiquesting Guide

## Section/Phase Hierarchy

1. **Phase 1: Tutorial & Early Lumbridge** — Foundation skills (Cooking 4, Crafting 2); enables Port Sarim/Al Kharid toll.
2. **Phase 2: Resource Gathering & GE Setup** — Prayer unlock; coin reward recycled for spade, first mine operation.
3. **Phase 3: Varrock Hub Clustering** — Parallel quest interleave; 11 QP checkpoint; skill unlocks (Magic 8, Mining 10).
4. **Phase 4: Combat & Crafting Nodes** — Regional spread; Draynor efficiency (2 quests, 1 trip); clay/ore banking.
5. **Phase 5: Northern Fortresses** — Medium quest complexity; 33 QP checkpoint.
6. **Phase 6: Icy Region & Smithing Breakpoint** — Knight's Sword gates Smithing 29; sequential Port Sarim trio.
7. **Phase 7: Final Skill Training & Dragon Slayer I** — Mandatory off-guide Magic 10→33, Prayer 11→37 interlude before final boss; 44 QP terminal.

## Task Grammar

Format: quest identifier, _prerequisite clause_, **action noun**, _target/location_, (skill outcome or unlock if leveling).

**Examples (paraphrased, ≤12 words):**
- *Start X Marks the Spot* — receive Prayer exp; coordinate coin spend at Lumbridge GE.
- *While Rune Mysteries walks north,* **collect knife** upstairs Varrock General Store — zero extra trip.
- *After Sheep Shearer coins,* **buy spade** Lumbridge store, begin digging for Tutorial treasure.
- *Imp Catcher requires 4 beads;* **kill imps on Karamja volcano** OR purchase at GE.
- *Draynor Manor exit:* **grab bronze med helm;** store for Black Knights' Fortress later.
- *Below Ice Mountain completion* → **bake redberry pie at Al Kharid bank** for Knight's Sword.

## Interleaving & Prep Wisdom

### Co-located Clustering
- **Draynor efficiency**: Ernest the Chicken + Vampyre Slayer share location; exit Manor via bronze med helm pickup (dual-use drop).
- **Varrock hub**: Romeo & Juliet, Demon Slayer, Rune Mysteries, Prince Ali start/progress in single bank visit. Upstairs knife grab eliminates future trip.
- **Port Sarim trio**: Knight's Sword + Witch's Potion + Corsair Curse chained back-to-back with giant-rat-meat interim kills.
- **Al Kharid corridor**: Prince Ali Rescue completed post–Below Ice Mountain; redberry pie prep at bank beforehand.

### Parallel Quest Layers
- **Start early, finish late**: Rune Mysteries, Restless Ghost, and Imp Catcher initialize during Phase 2 but complete in Phase 3. Restless Ghost ghost skull **stored in bank** after Imp Catcher to complete later.
- **Prince Ali split**: Romeo & Juliet provides 5 redberries (collected during Varrock visit). Redberries combined with flour at bank for pie, consumed at Al Kharid checkpoint.

### "Do X While Y" Patterns
- While walking Rune Mysteries north (Aubury) → **buy 4 onions, 2 iron bars, 2 iron ore at GE** for later quests.
- While passing Barbarian Village (Shield of Arrav) → **recruit Checkal for future-reminder** (Below Ice Mountain resource hint).
- While Demon Slayer boss-running → **collect mushrooms, herbs** en route for potion prep.
- While Below Ice Mountain climbs → **refill bucket of water** after Rune Mysteries for soft-clay production later.

### Skill-to-Breakpoint Ordering
- **Cooking 4** (Cook's Assistant) → **immediate redberry pie unlock** (Prophet Ali, Knight's Sword).
- **Crafting 2** (Sheep Shearer wool reward) → **spin wool into balls** (multi-quest armor filler).
- **Mining 10** (Doric's Quest) → **unlock clay/ore harvesting** at Varrock south mine; batch excavation during free time.
- **Smithing 29** (Knight's Sword reward) → **gates rune platebody/d'hide gear** for future progression; breaks F2P multiquesting terminal.
- **Magic 10** + **Prayer 11** → **mandatory training loop off-guide** before Dragon Slayer I (final 10→33 Magic, 11→37 Prayer).

### Ironman Variant: No GE Sourcing
- Kill imps repeatedly (Karamja volcano spawn) for 4-bead harvest instead of GE spend.
- Take 2+ additional potion bottles from Lumbridge Castle kitchen (pre-phase-1 loot).
- Collect cooked beef from raw beef roasted over spit instead of quest reward purchases.

## Steer-Point Orientation

### Quest Point Milestones (Terminal Progression Gate)
- **11 QP** (end Romeo & Juliet) — Halfway checkpoint; enables ~50% of F2P quest catalog.
- **33 QP** (end Demon Slayer) — Final quest before off-guide training; commits to Dragon Slayer I path.
- **44 QP** (Dragon Slayer I complete) — Terminal F2P multiquest goal; all 24 quests resolved.

### Skill Level Unlocks (Non-Combat, Leveling Gate)
- **Cooking 4** (Cook's Assistant XP) → Redberry pie baking.
- **Crafting 2** (Sheep Shearer XP) → Ball of wool spinning.
- **Magic 8** (Imp Catcher XP) → Rune Mysteries completion.
- **Mining 10** (Doric's Quest XP) → Clay/ore batch harvesting.
- **Prayer 11** (X Marks the Spot XP) → Early prayer training foundation (not used until Dragon Slayer off-guide interlude).
- **Smithing 29** (Knight's Sword XP) → Rune armor / d'hide unlock (gates future-tier gear for P2P progression).

### Regional Access Unlocks (Gating)
- **Al Kharid toll** (10 gp × 2 from Sheep Shearer coins) → Unlocks southern desert, Ali Rescue, Doric access.
- **Port Sarim jail via Shantay Pass teleport** (Below Ice Mountain completion) → Knight's Sword, Corsair Curse access.
- **Karamja boat** (Port Sarim dock post–Phase 6) → Imp bead collection (Ironman alt), demon boss location.

### Gear Tier Progression (Armor/Combat Caps)
- **Bronze start** (Tutorial Island sword + shield) → Ernest/Vampire/Demon early ranged + melee.
- **Iron requirement** (Black Knights' Fortress) → Bronze med helm + iron equipment (quest-gated obtain).
- **Rune unlock** (Smithing 29 via Knight's Sword XP) → Rune platebody, d'hide armor (post-F2P preparation).

### Off-Guide Mandatory Interlude (Before Dragon Slayer I)
- **Magic 10 → 33** (approx 5–8 hours solo fire-strike or splashing).
- **Prayer 11 → 37** (approx 2–4 hours chicken bone sacrifice or Ecto via Restless Ghost skull).
- These levels enable Dragon Slayer I survival (spellbook nuking, defensive prayer protection).

### Collection/Availability Steer Points (Non-Explicit)
- **4-bead consolidation** (Imp Catcher trade marker) — Drives ahead-of-time Karamja spawn or GE budget planning.
- **23-bone mass harvest** (Wizard Tower basement chickens) — Centralizes below-surface logistics (bank deposit, skull-store tactic).
- **Redberry-pie production gate** (Cooking 4, flour supply) — Batching timing affects Varrock hub exit efficiency.
- **Bronze-bar smelt requirement** (Misthalin Mystery activity) — Determines ore pre-collection before Doric quest start.

## Design Pattern for Unified Progression Burndown

This guide exemplifies **quest-skill interleaving under regional constraint**:
- Quests do NOT complete in order; they **layer and stall** awaiting skill unlocks (Magic 8, Smithing 29) or material pre-prep (23 bones, 4 beads).
- **Clustering minimizes travel**: Draynor 2-quest loop, Varrock hub 4-quest convergence, Port Sarim trio chaining.
- **Background loops (bone farming, imp-hunting, ore mining) run ahead-of-time**, not JIT, to compress final-phase quest-completion throughput.
- **Gear progression is gated, not fluid**: Bronze → Iron (quest-blocked) → Rune (skill-XP-blocked post-Dragon Slayer), creating natural training breakpoints.
- **Off-guide skill training (Magic, Prayer) is MANDATORY**, not optional, and should be front-loaded into the burndown calendar to avoid "stuck at final boss" bottleneck.
