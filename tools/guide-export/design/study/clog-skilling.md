# Collection Log Based Skilling — Structure & Requisites

## 1. Section/Phase Hierarchy

**Organizing Principle**: Skill-centric, not temporal phases. Each skill has 1–6 designated activities that grant BOTH skill XP AND collection log drops. Progression checkpoint = collection log completion % per activity + skill level milestones.

- **18 Skills** (Agility, Construction, Cooking, Farming, Firemaking, Fishing, Fletching, Herblore, Hunter, Magic, Mining, Prayer, Runecraft, Sailing, Smithing, Thieving, Woodcutting, + Pets)
- **Per-skill activities** (e.g., Fishing: Trawler, Tempoross, Aerial Fishing)
- **Milestones**: Skill level 99, collection log completion %, pet acquisition

No explicit episode/bank/region gating described; activities are largely skill-siloed and parallelizable.

## 2. Task Grammar

**One task format**:  
"Train SKILL at ACTIVITY/LOCATION to reach LEVEL, collecting ITEM drops; repeat until log completion."

**Examples** (≤12 words):
- Train Fishing at Tempoross, collect log items, reach 99.
- Complete Hallowed Sepulchre collection log while gaining Agility XP.
- Obtain Firemaking pet (Phoenix) via Wintertodt or Shades.
- Grind Volcanic Mine to 99 Mining and collect ore items.
- Acquire baby chinchompa pet at Hunter Guild during training.

## 3. Interleaving & Prep Wisdom

### Structure
- **Solo/parallel**: Each skill operates independently; no hard cross-skill prerequisites noted.
- **Pet hunting**: Interleaved with skill training (pets drop while grinding activities).
- **Background candidates** (inferred from activity types):
  - Tempoross (Fishing) — group activity, but repetitive; could batch sessions
  - Guardians of the Rift (Runecraft) — minigame, repeated runs
  - Wintertodt (Firemaking) — group activity, long sessions
  - Forestry (Woodcutting) — new activity, likely batched
- **Active/focused**: Hallowed Sepulchre (Agility), Tithe Farm (Farming), Giant's Foundry (Smithing) require sustained attention.

### Batching/Sequencing Rules
1. **Gear-gated activities first**: Activities requiring specific gear or unlock items should be sequenced AFTER obtaining that gear.
2. **Minigame fatigue**: Long minigames (Tempoross, Wintertodt, GotR) benefit from interspersed skill training in between.
3. **Pet drop cadence**: Pets are passive; accept them as they arrive during normal grinding.
4. **No "level gate then move" rule stated**: Guide does not specify "train to breakpoint X before attempting Y"; activities appear opt-in per player progression.

### Prep & Just-In-Time Logic
- **Supplies**: Most activities are self-contained (e.g., Tempoross rewards fishing supplies); no external GE-based supply chain implied.
- **Quest/diary requirements**: NOT DETAILED in this guide; flagged for unwinding.
- **Regional access**: Activities span Kourend, Kandarin, Tirannwn, Morytania, Karamja, etc.; no stated sequencing by region.

## 5. Steer-Point Orientation

**Primary steer points** (not just levels):
1. **Skill level 99** — terminal goal per skill; no mid-level gates enforced in this guide.
2. **Collection log completion %** — per activity; drives when activity is "done."
3. **Pet acquisition** — 9 skilling pets across 14+ skills; secondary milestone.
4. **Gear unlocks from log drops** — e.g., carpenter outfit (construction), fishing outfit (fishing); grants passive bonuses; unlock precedence not specified.

**Non-level, non-quest steer-points**:
- **Log completion breakpoints** (e.g., "50% → 100% Tempoross log").
- **Unique item drop gates** (e.g., pet or outfit item must drop before next phase).
- **Minigame performance tiers** (e.g., higher Zalcano infoboxes = more drop chance; speedrunning Hallowed Sepulchre for special loot).

**Missing from guide**: Specific "progress to unlock X region" or "quest Y required before activity Z" — these are flagged for downstream unwinding.
