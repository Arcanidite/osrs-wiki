# DunkingOreos Early Ironman/HCIM Starter Guide - Granularity Analysis

## Overview

A comprehensive early-game progression guide covering Tutorial Island through Recipe for Disaster (RFD). Structured into 5 major sections with ~35 numbered steps per section, decomposing into hundreds of atomic actions.

## Granularity Observations

### High-Atomic Decomposition

Most steps are broken to **single-click/action granularity**:

- "Go north to hall, take one cooked meat piece and burn it" (discrete location, single item, single action)
- "Buy 5 ropes from general store" (specific NPC, exact quantity)
- "Pick up 16 redberries and 1 cadava berries" (exact location, exact count)
- "Safespot by standing south of chair on EASTERN wall" (precise positioning for combat)
- "Minigame tele to Soul Wars, grab knife from bank, leave to Edgeville" (sequential sub-actions, each discrete)

### Exact Quantities Throughout

Nearly every action includes measurable targets:
- "Mine 7 clay by Champions' Guild"
- "Fish 200 karambwanji to feed kittens"
- "Get 20 limpwurt roots"
- "Make 3 waxwood planks"
- "Chop and light normal logs to 35 Woodcutting"
- "Craft 2 sapphire amulets and enchant ONE"

### Combat Specificity

Boss fights include exact tactical positioning and damage mechanics:
- "Safespot mother behind rock and use protect from missiles" (location + prayer + spell)
- "Pray melee before fight start and run west into same safespot as Dad" (prayer setup + movement path)
- "Max hit 32; go south of bridge and run west to have gnomes deal most damage" (predicted damage + tactical positioning)
- "Bring snares, water blast, ice gloves, gas mask" (exact inventory setup for specific NPC)

### NPC Dialogue Routing

Many steps specify exact dialogue options when multiple exist:
- "Talk to Hassan, then Osman" (ordered NPC sequence)
- "Talk to Captain Cain (dialogue option to skip tutorial)"
- "Continue Dragon Slayer I, ask all questions" + "Check quest guide to ensure all questions asked"

### Progression Gates & Checkpoints

Section structure uses major quest completion and skill level thresholds:

**Section 1: Ardougne Cloak** (9 steps)
- Subsection 1A: Starting Off (Steps 1–9, local region quests)
- Subsection 1B: Kandarin Part 1 (Steps 9–16, northbound quests + skills)
- Subsection 1C: Back to Asgarnia (Steps 18–27, early combat, mid-level quests)
- Subsection 1D: Kandarin Once More (Steps 29–35, unlocks for Section 2)

**Section 2: Minigames & Quests** (Tempoross → Wintertodt → Early Combat Quests)
- Checkpoint: ~58 Fishing (200 permits from Tempoross)
- Checkpoint: 50 Firemaking (Wintertodt grind)
- Checkpoint: Saph necklaces → Games necklaces (enchanting chain)

**Section 3: Skilling Grinds** (Hill Giants → Graceful Outfit unlock)
- Checkpoint: 46,512 Thieving XP / level 42 (Fruit stalls)
- Checkpoint: 260 marks of grace (Canifis Rooftop)
- Checkpoint: Graceful outfit acquired

**Section 4: Fairy Rings & Thieving**
- Checkpoint: Quest chain (Holy Grail → Eyes of Glouphrie → Fairytale I → Fairy Rings unlock)
- Checkpoint: 50 Thieving (Blackjacking for Ali's rune shop unlock)

**Section 5: Completing RFD** (35 steps, highest granularity)
- Checkpoint: Each RFD sub-quest completion
- Checkpoint: Skill thresholds (65 Cooking, 65 Attack/Strength, 37 Ranged)
- Terminal checkpoint: Dragon Defender acquisition (Warrior Guild)

### Optional vs. Mandatory Paths

Guide clearly marks alternatives without reducing atomic clarity:
- "If no sapphire necklaces obtained, craft up to 10" (condition, exact quantity)
- "If all else fails, sell rune items or jewelry to West Ardougne shop" (fallback action sequence)
- "Optional: Stay until 99; 90 required for master clue step" (branching, with condition)
- "If did NOT get 43 Prayer earlier, [HCIM warning] complete His Faithful Servants..." (prerequisite check, alternate path)

### Inventory & Banking Protocol

Many steps specify exact inventory management:
- "Bank everything except spade and gp"
- "Keep chocolate cake for A Tail of Two Cats"
- "Withdraw 10 planks and hammer on way back to Romeo"
- "Keep all golo tops and papayas" (selective collection during grinds)

### Item Gathering Methodology

All gathering is framed as **action chains**, never generic "buy from GE":
- Kill → loot → bank pattern (e.g., "Kill imps until all 4 beads collected")
- Skill unlock→ craft path (e.g., "Craft 2 sapphire amulets and enchant ONE into Amulet of Magic")
- NPCs & shops with exact buy sequences (e.g., "Vodka east side, rest west side" at Grand Tree)

### Skill Grinds with Multitasking

Grinding sections embed secondary objectives:
- "Chop and light teaks until 50 firemaking → Chop teaks until 58 woodcutting"
- "Raise kittens in process" (during Tempoross & Wintertodt)
- "Cook raw food on fire while waiting for run energy recharge" (during Rogue's Den)
- "Fletch 1005 bronze arrows while running laps" (Draynor Rooftop)

### HCIM-Specific Safety Callouts

Guide acknowledges Hardcore constraints with atomic precision:
- "[HCIM warning] complete His Faithful Servants with Air Blast for 20k prayer xp, safespotting melee brothers"
- "Max hit 5" markers on enemies to signal survivability
- Prayer setup & restore mechanics for high-damage encounters
- "Do not turn in next fully grown kitten; should have ~4k deaths by now" (resource management flag)

## Verb Taxonomy

**Navigation & Positioning:**
- walk-to, run-to, teleport (with exact spell/item), navigate (with landmarks), safespot

**NPCs & Dialogue:**
- talk-to, continue (quest dialogue), ask (all questions), start, complete

**Combat:**
- kill (N creatures, noted max hit), flinch, safespot, tag (for body block), pray (specific prayer), use (spell on target)

**Gathering & Inventory:**
- pick-up, grab, take, bank, withdraw, keep, drop, equip, carry

**Skilling & Crafting:**
- kill (for resources), mine (to level N), chop (to level N), fish (N creatures), cook (on range/fire), craft (recipe), smith, blow, fletch, enchant, tan, make (dyes, potions)

**Quests & Objectives:**
- complete (quest), dig (treasure), steal (items), pickpocket, read (notice board), inspect (objects), search (containers)

**Misc:**
- buy/purchase (exact qty, specific NPC), use (item on target), hop (worlds), restore (energy/stats at pool)

## Chunking & Progression Granularity

**Coarse Intent:** "Complete Ardougne Cloak"
**Unwinds Into:**
1. Complete 40+ individual quests across Lumbridge, Draynor, Falador, Varrock, Kandarin
2. Reach 20–40 Agility via rooftop courses
3. Reach 13+ Magic via safespotted barbarians (if needed)
4. Reach 35+ Woodcutting, 10+ Agility via skill grinds
5. Steal 2+ inventories of silk for cloak diary
6. Each of these breaks further into: NPC visits, combat encounters, item pickups, level checks

**Coarse Intent:** "Get 65 Cooking via wines"
**Unwinds Into:**
1. Fairy ring BIQ → run south to Bedabin Camp (navigation checkpoint)
2. Buy grapes at RFD chest (specific NPC, items)
3. Fill jugs of water at Hosidious Kitchen (repeated action, exact location)
4. Cook all grapes on range (batch action, single location)
5. Train to 70 Cooking via Mess Hall (secondary path if needed)

**Coarse Intent:** "Kill Elvarg in Dragon Slayer I"
**Unwinds Into:**
1. Enter Crandor volcano
2. Resurface (exits dungeon, specific mechanic)
3. Re-enter dungeon (inventory/preparation checkpoint)
4. Open dungeon shortcut before fighting (precise action order)
5. Reach Elvarg tile (movement precision)
6. Single-target boss kill (exact loot: head, 4 red spider eggs)
7. Bank items at Soul Wars
8. Secondary task: "Buy green d'hide body" → monastery bless

## Consistency Notes

- **No F2P/P2P split:** Guide treats progression as single continuum (F2P→members at Charter ship arrival)
- **Farming/Diary:** Ardougne Diary tasks are interleaved atomically, not grouped separately
- **Kittens/Cats:** Raised throughout (Tempoross, Wintertodt, finally turned into wily cat for spices)
- **World-hopping:** Explicitly called out for specific tasks ("Hop worlds, pick up 5 meat and 5 beers")
- **Prayer restoration:** Noted at specific locations (Ferox pool, temples, upon-tele checkpoints)

## Reusability for Automation

The granularity supports step-by-step execution:
- Each step can be unit-tested (NPC dialogue completion, item acquired, location reached)
- Exact quantities enable validation (inventory check: "have 16 redberries?")
- Safespots & positioning can be encoded as tile coordinates
- Skill level gates can be checked pre-step
- Quest completion can be checked via quest state machine
- Combat encounters include exact tactics and damage predictions
