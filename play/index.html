What you're walking on

The terrain is the real map from the RuneLite cache extraction (assets/data/cache/map/ — 1,150 regions, 64×64 tiles each, rendered at 4 px/tile, scaled with nearest-neighbour for the retro look). Movement is tick-true to the real game: one game tick every 600 ms, walking moves 1 tile/tick, running 2 tiles/tick (source: OSRS Wiki, “Game tick”).

What's missing, and exactly why

  • Collision — the map dump renders terrain colours only; tile movement flags weren't extracted, so nothing blocks you yet. (BACKLOG [client:collision-flags])
  • NPCs & objects in the world — locations.pack is empty until the XTEA keys are refreshed; we know what 12,076 NPCs are, not where they stand. (BACKLOG [client:locations-spawns])
  • The game itself — combat, quests, drops and AI run on Jagex servers. They are not present in RuneLite or the cache in any form, so a 1:1 standalone game cannot be extracted — only re-implemented against sourced formulas. (BACKLOG [client:simulation])

Source: RuneLite cache extraction (map tiles) + OSRS Wiki game-tick mechanics · stamp 2026-07-06. Region ids in this dump are internally coherent but do not match live OSRS region numbering (see tools/kb/GAME_GOTCHAS.md G-4).
