What this is

The world and its collision are decoded from the real game cache (OpenRS2 archive, cache 2499 · build 236 · 2026-03-18) by tools/openrs2_extract.py: terrain block flags, wall edges and corners, and object footprints honoring each object's clip type — the same static clipping rules the game applies. Movement is tick-true: one game tick every 600 ms, 1 tile/tick walking, 2 running; click-to-walk uses the game-style breadth-first search with no corner cutting (source: OSRS Wiki, “Game tick” / “Pathfinding”). Press C to see the collision overlay.

Objects are live: every interactable object placement (57,130 across the world) is decoded from the cache with its real right-click menu — hover shows the game-style “default action Name” text, right-click opens the option list exactly as the cache defines it, and clicking walks you there. Doors and gates genuinely open: opening clears exactly the wall edges their closed state clips, so buildings are navigable — the same passage semantics as the game.

Actions perform. Mining works on copper/tin/iron/coal rocks (wiki-sourced rock ids validated against the extracted mine placements; real level reqs, XP, and per-ore respawn times — rocks deplete after one ore as in the game). Chopping trees yields the real log items with the real Woodcutting XP and level requirements (Tree→Logs 25 xp, Oak 15/37.5, Willow 30/67.5, Maple 45/100, Yew 60/175, Magic 75/250 — OSRS Wiki, 2026-07-07), levels follow the documented XP curve, your 28-slot inventory and skills persist locally, and bank booths open a working deposit/withdraw bank. You start with a bronze axe (sandbox kit). Honesty note: Jagex has never published exact success/depletion/respawn rates — those four knobs are labelled placeholders in assets/js/world/gather.js (GATHER_CONFIG), and tree/axe item ids are test-verified against the real item cache. Actions without an implemented system still say so rather than faking an outcome.

Vertical movement works. All four floors and the underground are extracted (per-plane terrain, collision, and objects — 3,222 upper-floor region sheets). Staircases and ladders climb between floors; trapdoors and cellar ladders drop into the real mapped dungeons. Convention note: each climbable's exact destination tile is server-side data not present in the cache, so we use the game's documented coordinate conventions — same tile one plane up/down, and the standard 6,400-tile north offset for underground areas — then settle on the nearest walkable mapped tile (validated: the Lumbridge kitchen trapdoor lands in the real cellar beside its real exit ladder). Unmapped destinations refuse honestly instead of guessing.

NPCs are in the world. 18,888 spawn points (validated against the real NPC cache — Hans, the Cook and the tutors stand in Lumbridge where they should) render as markers with game-style hover text ("Talk-to Man (level-2)") and right-click menus listing each NPC's real cache actions. Spawn coordinates are server-side data, so they come from the community dataset behind the OSRS Wiki world map (mejrs/data_osrs, stamped in tools/kb/GAME_KB.md) — best-effort, may drift after game updates.

NPC interactions perform. Attack fights with the documented melee formulas over the real cache stats (max hit, accuracy rolls, 4 × damage combat XP + 1.33 × Hitpoints XP, 4-tick unarmed speed; you start at Hitpoints 10) — NPCs hit back, die, and respawn at their spawn point. Pickpocketing works where sourced (Man/Woman: 8 Thieving xp, 3 coins; failure stuns and hurts). Net fishing spots yield raw shrimps (10 Fishing xp; a small net is in the starting kit). Bankers bank. Altars restore Prayer points. Searching scenery gives the game's default "nothing of interest". Talk-to and Trade open a dialogue frame that says plainly that dialogue scripts and shop stock are server-side and unsourced — never invented. Success rates remain labelled approximations/placeholders in assets/js/world/combat.js.

Gear works. 2,229 equipable items carry their real bonuses (osrsbox-db equipment data, id+name test-verified against the item cache — items whose names drifted from this cache snapshot are dropped, never guessed). Click an equippable inventory item to Equip it; the Equipment tab shows worn gear per slot with summed bonuses, and combat swings genuinely use your stab attack + melee strength bonuses in the documented formulas.

Monsters drop loot. 2,086 NPCs have sourced drop tables (osrsbox-db, 58,375 entries; every item id+name validated against the item cache, rarities kept exactly as sourced). Kills roll the real table; loot lands on the ground as small red dots (Take on hover/click, ≈2-minute despawn). NPCs without a sourced table say so in the log and drop nothing — never invented.

NPCs wander. Ambient NPCs randomly walk within a bounded radius of their spawn point, respecting the same collision as you; fighting or dead NPCs stand still. Exact wander radius/cadence are unpublished server data — the Chebyshev-5 radius and ~1.8 s step are labelled placeholders in assets/js/world/npc-ai.js.

Still missing, and exactly why

  • Shop stock/prices — server-side data with no public sourced dataset (osrsbox-db and mejrs/data_osrs checked 2026-07-07 — none exists). BACKLOG: shop stock dataset needed (wiki-derived shops.json); Trade says so in-game rather than inventing a stock list. (BACKLOG [client:simulation])
  • Quests, dialogue scripts — run on Jagex servers and are not present in any client data; they can only be re-implemented from sourced formulas under the KB no-fabrication protocol. (BACKLOG [client:simulation])
